# SOP-LCC-002: Licensing and permits contact centre (voice and chat)

> **Fictitious document.** Marsa Public Services Authority, its services, fees, systems and staff titles are invented for demonstration. Nothing here describes a real authority or real law.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document id | SOP-LCC-002 |
| Version | 1.0 (synthetic) |
| Owner | Head of Licensing Contact Services, Marsa Public Services Authority |
| Related use case | UC01: AI voice contact centre for licensing and permits |
| Applies to | Inbound calls and chats about licences and permits: status enquiries, fees, required documents, choosing the right service, callbacks and written follow-up |
| Review cycle | Every 12 months |

## 1. Purpose

Give every caller a correct, consistent answer about Marsa licences and permits in their language, first time, and get anything the contact centre cannot answer to the right person quickly. Callers always know when they are speaking to an AI assistant and can ask for a person at any time. The contact centre informs and routes; it never decides an application, changes a record or takes a payment. Every written message to a citizen is released by the Contact Centre Supervisor before it is sent.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** inbound calls and web chats; application and case status; fees and documents for services in the Service and Fee Catalogue (section 6); guidance on which service fits the caller's need; warm transfers and callbacks; written follow-up messages to the caller's registered contact details.

**Out of scope:** deciding or changing any application (SOP-LIC-014 and the permit SOPs); taking payments; complaints (SOP-CMP-003); requests for a copy or export of personal data (SOP-DPA-002); outbound campaigns.

## 3. Roles

Four job profiles carry out this procedure. Each has a distinct purpose, a fixed set of tasks and its own limits.

### 3.1 Contact Centre Agent
- **Purpose:** answer the caller, verify identity where needed, give information from approved sources and route anything else.
- **Works with:** callers (voice and chat), the Licensing Case Officer, the Contact Centre Supervisor.
- **Authority:** may read case status in the Licensing Case System, quote the Service and Fee Catalogue, open an enquiry record, book a callback and draft a written message. May not decide, change a case or licence record, take a payment, promise an outcome, or send any written message without the Supervisor's release.
- **Skills:** clear speech and writing in Arabic and English, the catalogue, the status glossary (section 6.2), identity checks, calm handling of upset callers.

### 3.2 Licensing Case Officer
- **Purpose:** answer the questions the Agent hands over that need the case file or a licensing judgement.
- **Works with:** the Contact Centre Agent, the Licensing Supervisor named in SOP-LIC-014.
- **Authority:** may read and annotate the case, call the citizen back and explain the next step. Decisions stay with the Licensing Supervisor under SOP-LIC-014.
- **Skills:** licensing procedures, the case system, plain explanation of a case.

### 3.3 Contact Centre Quality Analyst
- **Purpose:** check that answers are correct and that the catalogue covers what callers ask.
- **Works with:** the Contact Centre Supervisor, the Director of Regulatory Procedures (catalogue owner).
- **Authority:** may score sampled contacts, log knowledge gaps and propose catalogue changes. May not change the catalogue.
- **Skills:** call evaluation, the scoring guide, root-cause notes.

### 3.4 Licensing Contact Centre Supervisor (Approving Officer)
- **Purpose:** release written messages, handle escalations and own the quality of the service.
- **Works with:** all three roles above, the Head of Licensing Contact Services.
- **Authority:** the only role that may release a written message to a citizen, approve an exception to this SOP, and close a complaint escalation at contact-centre level.
- **Skills:** the catalogue and licensing rules, judgement on exceptions, coaching.

## 4. Procedure

Task ids are used in the audit log and the training material.

### Step 1. Contact and identification (Contact Centre Agent)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T01 | Answer the contact. State the AI disclosure, that the call is recorded, and offer Arabic or English. Offer a person at any time. |
| T02 | Find out what the caller needs and match it to a catalogue service or a status enquiry. |
| T03 | For anything about a specific case or licence, verify the caller's identity (section 10, D2) before giving any case detail. |

### Step 2. Answer (Contact Centre Agent)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T04 | Status enquiry: read the case status from the Licensing Case System and explain it using the status glossary. |
| T05 | Fees and documents: quote the fee and the document list exactly from the Service and Fee Catalogue. If a service or fee is not in the catalogue, say so and offer a callback. |
| T06 | Service navigation: tell the caller which service fits their need and how to apply, citing the catalogue entry. |

### Step 3. Handover (Agent, Licensing Case Officer)

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T07 | Transfer to a person at once when the caller asks, is distressed, or raises a complaint. | Contact Centre Agent |
| T08 | When the answer needs the case file or a judgement, book a callback and write a handover note. | Contact Centre Agent |
| T09 | Call the citizen back and answer, or explain the next step. | Licensing Case Officer |

### Step 4. Written follow-up (Agent, Supervisor)

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T10 | When a caller asks for something in writing, draft the message to their registered contact details only. | Contact Centre Agent |
| T11 | Check the draft against the record and the catalogue, then release or return it. | Contact Centre Supervisor |
| T12 | Send the released message and record it on the enquiry. | Contact Centre Agent |

### Step 5. Close and assure

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T13 | Record the outcome of the contact and close the enquiry. | Contact Centre Agent |
| T14 | Log any question the catalogue could not answer as a knowledge gap. | Contact Centre Agent |
| T15 | Each week, score a 2% sample of contacts against this SOP. | Quality Analyst |
| T16 | Each month, review the sample results, gaps and escalations and report to the Head of Licensing Contact Services. | Contact Centre Supervisor |

## 6. Service and fee catalogue and status glossary (fictitious)

### 6.1 Service and fee catalogue

| Service | Fee | Documents (summary) | Procedure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trade licence renewal | By activity group: Retail and trading 1,200; Food and hospitality 2,000; Professional services 1,500; Industrial 3,000; plus 10% late fee within 30 days of expiry | Trade name certificate, lease, owner identity, insurance | SOP-LIC-014 |
| Signage permit | 800 per sign | Licence number, sign drawing with dimensions, landlord consent | SOP-PRM-004 |
| Outdoor seating permit | 1,000 per year | Licence number, site plan, landlord consent | SOP-PRM-006 |
| Business parking permit | 300 per vehicle per year | Licence number, vehicle registration | SOP-PRM-008 |

Amounts are in a synthetic currency unit. Fees are quoted exactly as in this table. A service that is not listed here has no fee the contact centre can quote.

### 6.2 Status glossary

| Status | What to tell the caller |
|---|---|
| Received | The application has arrived and is waiting for the pre-check. |
| On hold | Information is missing; an email to the registered address lists it. |
| Blocked | The application cannot continue under the normal procedure; a supervisor will decide. |
| Fee due | A fee notice has been sent; payment is needed before a decision. |
| Approved | The application has been approved; the licence or permit will be issued. |
| Issued | The licence or permit has been issued and is valid. |
| Rejected | The application was not approved; the notice gives reasons and the appeal route. |

## 7. Controls

1. Every contact starts with the AI disclosure; a caller who asks for a person is transferred without argument.
2. No case detail is given before identity is verified (D2).
3. Fees, documents and statuses are taken from the catalogue and the case system only; nothing is estimated.
4. A question the catalogue does not answer is not guessed; it is handed over and logged as a gap.
5. Every written message to a citizen is released by the Supervisor before it is sent, and goes only to the registered contact details on the record.
6. The contact centre does not take payments, change records, or export personal data.
7. Every contact is recorded with who, what and when.

## 8. Exceptions and escalation

| Situation | Handled by | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| Caller asks for a person | Agent | Next available person, at once |
| Complaint | Agent | Supervisor, then SOP-CMP-003 |
| Request for a copy or export of personal data | Agent | Data Protection Officer (SOP-DPA-002) |
| Identity cannot be verified | Agent | Counter visit with identity document |
| Caller threatens harm or reports danger | Agent | Supervisor and emergency services, at once |
| Suspected fraud or impersonation | Agent | Supervisor, then Compliance |

## 9. Records

Contact recordings and transcripts, enquiry records, handover notes, released messages, weekly sample scores, monthly report and knowledge-gap log. Recordings are kept for 12 months; other records for 7 years (synthetic retention periods).

## 10. Required documents and how to submit

The contact centre does not take documents. It needs only these items, depending on the request.

### D1. Case number or licence number
- **What it is:** the number given when the case was opened, or the number on the licence.
- **When needed:** any status enquiry or question about a specific case.

### D2. Identity verification
- **What it is:** the case or licence number plus two of: the registered mobile number (the call comes from it, or a one-time code is sent to it), the last four digits of the owner's identity document, the trade name exactly as licensed.
- **What must be true:** both items match the record.

### D3. Representative's authority
- **What it is:** an authorisation letter already on the case file (SOP-LIC-014, D5).
- **When needed:** a person other than the licence owner asks about a case.

### SUB. How to submit documents
Documents for an application go through the portal or the counter as the service's own SOP says. The Agent explains how; it never accepts documents by phone or chat.

### VAL. When verification on file is still valid
Within one chat session, or one call, identity verified once is not asked again. A new contact needs a new verification.

## 11. Detailed description of each task

### T01. Answer the contact and disclose
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** a call or chat arrives.
- **Steps:** greet the caller; state that they are speaking to Marsa's AI assistant (where the AI answers) and that the call is recorded; ask for Arabic or English; say they can ask for a person at any time.
- **Output:** a contact with the disclosure and language recorded.
- **Problems:** a caller who declines AI help is transferred at once (T07).

### T02. Find out the need and route it
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the disclosure is done.
- **Steps:** ask what the caller wants; match it to a status enquiry (T04), a catalogue question (T05), service navigation (T06) or an out-of-scope matter (section 2).
- **Output:** the contact routed to a task, or the caller told which channel applies.

### T03. Verify identity
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the caller asks about a specific case or licence.
- **Steps:** ask for D1 and the two items in D2; compare with the record; for a representative check D3 is on file.
- **Output:** identity verified, or not verified with the reason.
- **Problems:** after two failed attempts, give no case detail and tell the caller to visit the counter with their identity document.

### T04. Explain the status
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** identity is verified.
- **Inputs:** the case in the Licensing Case System (read only), the status glossary (section 6.2).
- **Steps:** read the status and the date of the last action; explain it with the glossary wording; if "On hold", tell the caller the email lists what is missing; give the next expected step and its timing from the service's SOP.
- **Output:** the caller told the status and next step.
- **Problems:** a status not in the glossary, or a case that looks wrong, goes to the Licensing Case Officer (T08). The Agent never changes the status.

### T05. Quote fees and documents
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the caller asks what a service costs or needs.
- **Steps:** find the service in section 6.1; quote the fee and documents exactly; for trade licence renewal, ask the activity group and whether the licence has expired, then quote by the table.
- **Output:** the fee and documents quoted, with the catalogue entry named.
- **Problems:** a service not in the catalogue (for example a special-event or mobile-kiosk permit) has no fee to quote: say the fee is not available in the catalogue, offer a callback (T08) and log a gap (T14).

### T06. Guide to the right service
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the caller is unsure which service they need.
- **Steps:** ask what they want to do; suggest the matching catalogue service and how to apply; name the catalogue entry.
- **Output:** the caller knows which service to apply for and how.
- **Problems:** if two services might fit, name both and offer a callback rather than choose.

### T07. Transfer to a person
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the caller asks for a person, is distressed, or complains.
- **Steps:** tell the caller they are being transferred; pass a short summary so they do not repeat themselves.
- **Output:** a warm transfer recorded on the enquiry.

### T08. Book a callback and hand over
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the answer needs the case file, a licensing judgement, or is not in the catalogue.
- **Steps:** tell the caller a Licensing Case Officer will call within 1 working day; write a handover note with the question, the case number and what was already said.
- **Output:** a callback booked and a handover note on the enquiry.

### T09. Call back and answer
- **Job profile:** Licensing Case Officer.
- **Starts when:** a handover note arrives.
- **Steps:** read the case; call the registered number; answer or explain the next step; record the result.
- **Output:** the question answered, recorded on the enquiry.
- **Problems:** a question that needs a decision goes to the Licensing Supervisor under SOP-LIC-014.

### T10. Draft a written message
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the caller asks for a confirmation, a summary or a notification in writing.
- **Steps:** draft the message in the caller's language; address it to the registered mobile or email on the record only, never to a number or address given on the call; include the case number.
- **Output:** a draft waiting for release.
- **Problems:** the Agent tells the caller the message will be sent after review, not at once.

### T11. Release the written message
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Supervisor.
- **Steps:** check every fact against the case and the catalogue; check the recipient is the registered contact; release, or return with the correction.
- **Output:** a released or returned message.
- **Time target:** the same working day.

### T12. Send the released message
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Steps:** send exactly the released text; record it on the enquiry.
- **Output:** a sent message on the record.

### T13. Close the enquiry
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Steps:** record the outcome, the language, any transfer or callback, and close the enquiry.
- **Output:** a closed enquiry with a complete record.

### T14. Log a knowledge gap
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** a question could not be answered from the catalogue or the glossary.
- **Steps:** record the question, the service named and the date.
- **Output:** an entry in the knowledge-gap log.

### T15. Weekly quality sample
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Quality Analyst.
- **Steps:** select 2% of contacts at random; score disclosure, verification, accuracy against the catalogue, handover and tone; note each deviation.
- **Output:** the weekly score sheet.

### T16. Monthly review
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Supervisor.
- **Steps:** review the scores, the gaps and the escalations; send proposed catalogue changes to the Director of Regulatory Procedures; report to the Head of Licensing Contact Services.
- **Output:** the monthly report.

## 12. Decision rules for a contact

### R1. Who decides
The contact centre decides nothing about an application. Only the roles named in the service's own SOP decide. The Contact Centre Supervisor decides only on releasing a written message and on exceptions to this SOP.

### R2. What may be said without verification
General information from the catalogue (fees, documents, how to apply, timings) may be given to anyone. Nothing about a specific case is given until T03 succeeds.

### R3. Quoting
A fee, document list or status is quoted only from section 6 or the case system. If it is not there, the answer is that it is not available, with a callback.

### R4. Written messages
A written message goes only to the registered contact details and only after the Supervisor's release. "Text me when it's ready" is a request for a written message and follows T10 to T12.

### R5. Personal data
The contact centre does not send, read out in full or export a person's record. A request for a copy goes to SOP-DPA-002.

### R6. Transfer
A request for a person is always granted.

### R7. Statuses and what they mean
The statuses in section 6.2 are the only ones the Agent explains. "On hold" means missing information; it is not an inspection hold.

## 13. Questions callers ask, and the answer under this SOP

### Q1. Am I speaking to a person?
No, this is Marsa's AI assistant. You can ask for a person at any time.

### Q2. What is the status of my application?
Once your identity is confirmed, the assistant reads the status from the case system and explains it and the next step.

### Q3. How much is a signage permit?
800 per sign.

### Q4. What does a special-event permit cost?
That permit is not in the catalogue, so no fee can be quoted. A Licensing Case Officer can call you back within 1 working day.

### Q5. Can you text me when my permit is ready?
A written message can be sent to your registered mobile after a supervisor has checked it, the same working day.

### Q6. Can you send me a copy of my full record?
Not through the contact centre. You can request a copy of your personal data through the data access procedure, on the portal or at the counter.

### Q7. Can I pay by phone?
No. Pay on the portal, by bank transfer quoting the case number, or at the counter.
