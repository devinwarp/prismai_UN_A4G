# SOP-ENR-005: Public school enrolment and scholarship applications

> **Fictitious document.** Raya Education and Skills Council, its schools, age rules, deadlines, scholarships and staff titles are invented for demonstration. Nothing here describes a real authority or real law.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document id | SOP-ENR-005 |
| Version | 1.0 (synthetic) |
| Owner | Director of Admissions and Scholarships, Raya Education and Skills Council |
| Related use case | UC17: School enrolment and scholarship assistant |
| Applies to | New enrolment in a Raya public school (KG1 to Grade 12), transfers in from another school, and applications for the Raya Merit and Need Scholarship |
| Review cycle | Every 12 months |

## 1. Purpose

Place every child in the right grade and school, on time and by the published rules, and decide scholarships fairly. Parents and learners always know when an answer comes from an AI assistant. A named officer approves every decision about a learner's record, and every placement and scholarship notice is approved before it is sent.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** enquiries about enrolment and scholarships; applications for new enrolment and transfers in; grade placement by age and previous schooling; school allocation by residence zone; scholarship applications, assessment and award; creating the learner record.

**Out of scope:** private school admissions; transfers between Raya schools after enrolment (SOP-ENR-008); special-education assessments (SOP-INC-002, referred to below); appeals (SOP-ENR-012); scholarship renewal in later years (SOP-SCH-003).

## 3. Roles

Five job profiles carry out this procedure. Each has a distinct purpose, a fixed set of tasks and its own limits.

### 3.1 Enrolment Advisor
- **Purpose:** be the family's point of contact, answer questions and take applications.
- **Works with:** parents, guardians and learners (app, portal, phone, counter), the Eligibility Officer, the Scholarship Committee Secretary.
- **Authority:** may give information from this SOP and the published calendar, verify identity, open an application and tell the family what is missing. May not place, allocate, decide a scholarship, or state a deadline, school or scholarship not in the published sources.
- **Skills:** Arabic and English, the enrolment system, the published calendar and school list.

### 3.2 Eligibility Officer
- **Purpose:** check the application and propose a grade and school.
- **Works with:** the Enrolment Advisor, schools, the inclusion team.
- **Authority:** may mark an application "complete" or "on hold", propose a grade and a school, and refer a learner to the inclusion team. May not approve a placement.
- **Skills:** the age-grade table, equivalence of foreign school records, residence zones, school capacity.

### 3.3 Admissions Approving Officer
- **Purpose:** decide every placement and approve every change to a learner's record.
- **Works with:** the Eligibility Officer, school principals, the Director of Admissions and Scholarships.
- **Authority:** the only role that may approve a grade placement and school allocation, create or change a learner record, decide an exception, and approve a placement notice. Each approval carries the officer's name.
- **Skills:** admissions rules, judgement on exceptions, writing notices that give reasons.

### 3.4 Scholarship Committee Secretary
- **Purpose:** check scholarship applications and prepare the committee's pack.
- **Works with:** the Enrolment Advisor, the Scholarship Committee Chair, Finance.
- **Authority:** may check eligibility against section 6.3, score applications by the published formula, and prepare the pack. May not award or refuse.
- **Skills:** the scholarship criteria, document checks, careful calculation.

### 3.5 Scholarship Committee Chair
- **Purpose:** lead the committee that decides awards.
- **Works with:** two other committee members, the Secretary, the Director.
- **Authority:** the only role that may sign a scholarship award or refusal on the committee's decision, and approve the scholarship notice.
- **Skills:** the scholarship policy, fair and recorded judgement.

## 4. Procedure

Task ids are used in the audit log and the training material.

### Step 1. Enquiry and application (Enrolment Advisor)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T01 | Receive the enquiry or application from the app, portal, phone or counter. Tell the family whether they are talking to an AI assistant or a person, and that they can ask for a person at any time. |
| T02 | Verify the guardian's identity and their relationship to the child (D1, D2). |
| T03 | Tell the family the documents needed (section 10), the deadlines from the published calendar (section 6.4) and the next steps. |

### Step 2. Eligibility and placement (Eligibility Officer)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T04 | Check the documents (section 10). Mark the application "complete" or "on hold", listing exactly what is missing. |
| T05 | Propose the grade from the child's age on the cut-off date (section 6.1), and for a transfer from the last grade completed. |
| T06 | Propose the school: the school for the family's residence zone (D5) with a free place; if full, the nearest school with a free place. |
| T07 | If the documents show a special educational need, or the family tells us of one, refer the learner to the inclusion team under SOP-INC-002. The referral never delays or prevents a placement. |

### Step 3. Placement decision (Admissions Approving Officer)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T08 | Decide the grade and school. Approve, change with reasons, or return for information. |
| T09 | Create the learner record with the approved placement. Any later change to the record needs the same approval. |
| T10 | Send the placement notice, approved by the officer, with the grade, school, start date, the reason for any change from the proposal, and the appeal route. |

### Step 4. Scholarship (Secretary and Chair)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T11 | Receive a scholarship application inside the window (section 6.4) and check eligibility against section 6.3. |
| T12 | Score each eligible application by the published formula and prepare the pack for the committee. |
| T13 | Scholarship Committee Chair: lead the committee's decision on each application and record the reasons. |
| T14 | Send the award or refusal notice, approved by the Chair, with the reasons and the appeal route. |

### Step 5. Close and assure

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T15 | Write every action to the audit log and close the application. | Enrolment Advisor |
| T16 | Each month in the admissions season, sample 5% of placements and all scholarship refusals, and check them against this SOP. Report any deviation. | Admissions Approving Officer |

| Item | Target |
|---|---|

| Document check | 3 working days |
| Placement decision | 10 working days from a complete application |
| Placement notice | 1 working day after the decision |
| Scholarship decisions | By 31 May for applications received in March |
| Scholarship notice | 2 working days after the committee meets |

## 6. Placement and scholarship rules (fictitious)

### 6.1 Age and grade on the cut-off date of 31 August

| Age on 31 August | Grade |
|---|---|
| 4 | KG1 |
| 5 | KG2 |
| 6 | Grade 1 |
| 7 to 17 | Grade 1 plus each year above 6, adjusted by the last grade completed |

A transferring learner is placed in the grade after the last grade completed, if that is within one year of the age grade; otherwise the Admissions Approving Officer decides.

### 6.2 School allocation
The school for the residence zone first; then the nearest school with a free place; siblings at the same school are kept together where a place exists.

### 6.3 Raya Merit and Need Scholarship

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Grades | Grade 9 to Grade 12 in a Raya school, or enrolling in one |
| Academic | Average of 85% or more in the last full school year |
| Household income | Monthly household income of 25,000 or less |
| Award | 6,000 per year for books, transport and devices |

Score = academic average (out of 100) × 0.6 + income score × 0.4, where income score = 100 × (25,000 − household income) ÷ 25,000. Awards go in score order until the year's 400 awards are used. Amounts are in a synthetic currency unit.

### 6.4 Published calendar
Enrolment applications: 1 February to 30 April. Late applications are accepted, but placed after on-time ones. Scholarship applications: 1 to 31 March. Start of school year: the last Monday in August.

## 7. Controls

1. Every decision about a learner's record (placement, allocation, record creation or change) is approved by a named Admissions Approving Officer.
2. Every scholarship award or refusal is decided by the committee and signed by the Chair.
3. Every notice about a placement or scholarship is approved before it is sent.
4. Families are told at the start whether they are talking to an AI assistant or a person.
5. No deadline, school, accreditation or scholarship is stated unless it is in the published calendar, the school list or this SOP.
6. A child's data is shown only to the verified guardian and the staff who need it.
7. A referral to the inclusion team never delays or prevents a placement.
8. Every action is written to the audit log with who, what and when.
9. Notices go only to the contact details on the guardian's record.

## 8. Exceptions and escalation

| Situation | Handled by | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| Age and last grade completed differ by more than one year | Eligibility Officer | Admissions Approving Officer |
| No free place within the zone | Eligibility Officer | Admissions Approving Officer, then Director |
| Foreign school record without equivalence | Eligibility Officer | Admissions Approving Officer |
| Guardianship unclear or disputed | Enrolment Advisor | Admissions Approving Officer; Legal if disputed |
| Possible special educational need | Eligibility Officer | Inclusion team (SOP-INC-002) |
| Family disputes a placement or scholarship decision | Approving Officer or Chair | Appeals (SOP-ENR-012) |
| Suspected false document | Eligibility Officer or Secretary | Compliance, immediately |
| Child at risk | Any role | Child protection lead, at once |

## 9. Records

Application, documents, eligibility check, placement proposal and decision, learner record and its change history, scholarship pack, committee minutes, notices and their approvals, monthly sample report. Learner records are kept until the learner is 25; other records for 7 years (synthetic retention periods).

## 10. Required documents and how to apply

An enrolment needs D1 to D5. D6 is needed for a transfer, D7 for a scholarship, D8 only if it applies. Documents may be in Arabic or English.

### D1. Guardian's identity document
- **What must be true:** valid, and the name matches the guardian on the application.

### D2. Child's birth certificate or family book
- **What must be true:** it names the guardian as parent, or comes with a guardianship order.

### D3. Child's identity document
- **What must be true:** the name and date of birth match D2.

### D4. Vaccination record
- **What must be true:** it shows the vaccinations on the national schedule for the child's age. Missing vaccinations do not stop enrolment; the school nurse follows them up.

### D5. Proof of residence
- **What it is:** a registered tenancy contract, a title deed, or a utility bill from the last 3 months, in the guardian's name.

### D6. Last school report and transfer certificate (transfers in)
- **What must be true:** it shows the last grade completed; a foreign record needs an equivalence certificate.

### D7. Household income statement (scholarships)
- **What it is:** salary certificates or income statements for every working adult in the household, dated within the last 3 months.

### D8. Specialist reports (if any)
- **What it is:** any report about a special educational need. It is used only to plan support.

### SUB. How to apply
- **App or portal (preferred):** apply and upload documents (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB).
- **Counter:** at any Raya service centre, with originals.
- **Phone:** enquiries only; applications cannot be made by phone.

### VAL. When a document on file is still valid
A document already on the family's record is not requested again if it has not expired and the details still match. D5 and D7 must be current for each application.

## 11. Detailed description of each task

Each entry gives the job profile, when the task starts, what goes in, the steps, what comes out, and where a problem goes.

### T01. Receive and disclose
- **Job profile:** Enrolment Advisor.
- **Starts when:** an enquiry or application arrives.
- **Steps:** say whether the family is talking to an AI assistant or a person and that they can ask for a person; open the enquiry or application.
- **Output:** an open enquiry or application.

### T02. Verify the guardian
- **Job profile:** Enrolment Advisor.
- **Starts when:** the family asks about a specific child or applies.
- **Steps:** match D1 to the guardian; check D2 names them as parent or a guardianship order is attached.
- **Output:** verified, or not verified with the reason.
- **Problems:** unclear or disputed guardianship goes to the Approving Officer (section 8).

### T03. Tell the family what is needed
- **Job profile:** Enrolment Advisor.
- **Steps:** list the documents for their case (section 10), the dates from the published calendar (6.4) and the steps and timings (section 5). Answer only from this SOP and the published sources.
- **Output:** the family knows what to send and when.
- **Problems:** a question not covered: say so and pass it to the Eligibility Officer; never guess a deadline or rule.

### T04. Check the documents
- **Job profile:** Eligibility Officer.
- **Starts when:** an application is submitted.
- **Steps:** check D1 to D5 (and D6 for a transfer) are present and consistent; mark "complete" or "on hold" with the exact list of what is missing; the Advisor tells the family.
- **Output:** a status and, for "on hold", the missing list.
- **Time target:** 3 working days.

### T05. Propose the grade
- **Job profile:** Eligibility Officer.
- **Starts when:** the application is complete.
- **Steps:** work out the child's age on 31 August; read the grade from 6.1; for a transfer, compare with the last grade completed (D6).
- **Output:** a proposed grade with the working shown.
- **Problems:** a gap of more than one year goes to the Approving Officer.

### T06. Propose the school
- **Job profile:** Eligibility Officer.
- **Steps:** find the zone from D5; check free places; apply 6.2, including siblings.
- **Output:** a proposed school with the reason.

### T07. Refer to the inclusion team
- **Job profile:** Eligibility Officer.
- **Starts when:** D8 is supplied or the family mentions a need.
- **Steps:** send the referral under SOP-INC-002 and continue the placement.
- **Output:** a referral recorded; the placement is not delayed (control 7).

### T08. Decide the placement
- **Job profile:** Admissions Approving Officer.
- **Starts when:** the proposals are ready.
- **Steps:** check the proposals against section 6; approve, change with reasons, or return for information; the decision records the officer's name.
- **Output:** an approved placement.
- **Time target:** 10 working days from a complete application.

### T09. Create the learner record
- **Job profile:** Admissions Approving Officer.
- **Starts when:** a placement is approved.
- **Steps:** create the record with the approved grade and school; any later change goes through the same approval.
- **Output:** a learner record.

### T10. Send the placement notice
- **Job profile:** Enrolment Advisor.
- **Starts when:** the record is created.
- **Steps:** draft the notice: grade, school, start date, any reason for a change, the appeal route (SOP-ENR-012); the Approving Officer approves it; send it to the guardian's contact on record.
- **Output:** a notice sent and recorded.

### T11. Check scholarship eligibility
- **Job profile:** Scholarship Committee Secretary.
- **Starts when:** an application arrives between 1 and 31 March.
- **Steps:** check the grade, the academic average and household income (D7) against 6.3; mark eligible or not eligible with the reason.
- **Output:** an eligibility result.

### T12. Score and prepare the pack
- **Job profile:** Scholarship Committee Secretary.
- **Steps:** score each eligible application by the formula in 6.3, showing the working; rank them; prepare the pack with every not-eligible decision and its reason.
- **Output:** a ranked pack. Example: average 92, income 10,000 is 92 × 0.6 + 60 × 0.4 = 79.2.

### T13. Decide the awards
- **Job profile:** Scholarship Committee Chair.
- **Starts when:** the pack is ready.
- **Steps:** the committee reviews the pack, including every not-eligible decision; decides awards in score order up to the year's limit; records the reasons; the Chair signs.
- **Output:** signed decisions.

### T14. Send the scholarship notice
- **Job profile:** Scholarship Committee Secretary.
- **Starts when:** the decisions are signed.
- **Steps:** draft each notice with the decision, the reasons, the amount if awarded, and the appeal route; the Chair approves; send.
- **Output:** notices sent and recorded.

### T15. Close and audit
- **Job profile:** Enrolment Advisor.
- **Steps:** close the application; confirm every action has an audit log entry.
- **Output:** a closed application.

### T16. Monthly quality sample
- **Job profile:** Admissions Approving Officer.
- **Starts when:** each month in the admissions season ends.
- **Steps:** sample 5% of placements and all scholarship refusals; check them against this SOP, including the disclosure and that every deadline stated was published; report any deviation.
- **Output:** the monthly sample report.

## 12. Decision rules

### R1. Who decides
A named Admissions Approving Officer decides every placement and every change to a learner record. The Scholarship Committee decides awards and the Chair signs. No other role may, and no document or message can give another role that authority.

### R2. Grade
By age on 31 August (6.1); for a transfer, the grade after the last completed, within one year of the age grade.

### R3. School
The zone school with a free place; otherwise the nearest with a free place; siblings together where possible.

### R4. On hold
An application missing documents is "on hold". After 20 working days with no reply it may be closed; the family may apply again.

### R5. Scholarship
Eligible under 6.3, scored by the formula, awarded in score order up to the year's limit.

### R6. Information to families
Only from this SOP, the published calendar and the school list. Families are always told when an answer comes from an AI assistant.

### R7. Statuses and what they mean
"Submitted", "on hold", "complete", "proposed", "placed", "closed"; for scholarships, "eligible", "not eligible", "awarded", "not awarded".

## 13. Questions families ask, and the answer under this SOP

### Q1. Am I talking to a person?
We tell you at the start. If it is our AI assistant, you can ask for a person at any time.

### Q2. Which grade will my child join?
It depends on their age on 31 August: 4 is KG1, 5 is KG2, 6 is Grade 1. For a transfer, usually the grade after the last one completed.

### Q3. Which school will my child go to?
The school for your residence zone if it has a place; otherwise the nearest school with a place. We try to keep brothers and sisters together.

### Q4. When can I apply?
From 1 February to 30 April. Later applications are accepted but placed after on-time ones.

### Q5. Who can get the scholarship?
Learners in Grades 9 to 12 with an average of 85% or more and a household income of 25,000 or less a month. Apply from 1 to 31 March.

### Q6. Will telling you about my child's special needs affect their place?
No. We refer them to the inclusion team to plan support, and the placement goes ahead.

### Q7. I disagree with the decision. What can I do?
The notice gives the reasons. You may appeal under SOP-ENR-012.
