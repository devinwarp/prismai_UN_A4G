# SOP-CSC-007: Voice contact centre for licensing and permits

> **Fictitious document.** Marsa Public Services Authority, its phone lines, service levels, systems and staff titles are invented for demonstration. Nothing here describes a real authority or real law.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document id | SOP-CSC-007 |
| Version | 1.0 (synthetic) |
| Owner | Head of Customer Service, Marsa Public Services Authority |
| Related use case | UC01: AI voice contact centre for licensing and permits |
| Applies to | Inbound calls to the licensing and permits line, the follow-up messages sent after a call, and callbacks |
| Review cycle | Every 12 months |

## 1. Purpose

Answer every caller about licences and permits quickly, correctly and in their language, and send them to the right service or person. The contact centre informs and routes; it never decides a licence, a permit or a fee. Every written follow-up that reaches a caller is checked by a person before it is sent.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** inbound calls on the licensing and permits line; general information about licensing services; case status for a verified caller; guidance to the right service and documents; callbacks; written follow-up messages (SMS or email) after a call; complaints received by phone.

**Out of scope:** decisions on licences, permits, fees, waivers or exceptions (handled by the licensing procedures, for example SOP-LIC-014 for renewals); appeals (SOP-LIC-021); outbound campaigns; calls about other authorities' services, which are only redirected.

## 3. Roles

Four job profiles carry out this procedure. Each has a distinct purpose, a fixed set of tasks and its own limits.

### 3.1 Contact Centre Agent
- **Purpose:** answer the call, find out what the caller needs, and answer or route it.
- **Works with:** callers, the Licensing Case Officer, the Contact Centre Team Leader.
- **Authority:** may give information from the approved answer library and the case system, open a callback request, draft a follow-up message and log the call. May not decide, promise an outcome, waive a fee, change a record or send a written message without release.
- **Skills:** spoken Arabic and English (Hindi and Urdu through the language line), identity verification, case-system lookup, the approved answer library, calm handling of upset callers.

### 3.2 Licensing Case Officer
- **Purpose:** handle callbacks that need someone who works on licence cases.
- **Works with:** the Agent, callers, the Licensing Supervisor under SOP-LIC-014.
- **Authority:** may explain a case in detail, take a request into the licensing procedure, and call the caller back. May not decide a case outside the licensing procedure that governs it.
- **Skills:** licensing procedures, case-system entry, explaining a decision and its reasons.

### 3.3 Contact Centre Team Leader (Approving Officer)
- **Purpose:** release written follow-ups, take over difficult calls, and own complaints.
- **Works with:** Agents, Case Officers, the Head of Customer Service for escalations.
- **Authority:** the only role that may release a written message to a caller, accept a complaint for investigation, and close a complaint.
- **Skills:** the approved answer library and the licensing procedures, judgement on unusual calls, writing clear replies in Arabic and English.

### 3.4 Quality and Knowledge Analyst
- **Purpose:** keep the approved answer library correct and check that calls follow this SOP.
- **Works with:** process owners in Licensing, the Team Leader.
- **Authority:** may add, change or retire an answer in the library once the process owner has approved the wording. May not answer callers.
- **Skills:** reading SOPs, call evaluation, writing plain answers.

## 4. Procedure

Task ids are used in the audit log and the training material.

### Step 1. Answer and identify (Contact Centre Agent)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T01 | Answer the call. Say that the call is with the Marsa licensing line, that an automated assistant may answer, and that the call is recorded. Offer Arabic or English, and the language line for Hindi and Urdu. |
| T02 | Find out what the caller needs and put the call in one category from section 6. |
| T03 | For any question about a specific case or licence, verify the caller's identity (section 10, D1 to D3) before giving case details. |

### Step 2. Serve (Contact Centre Agent)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T04 | Answer a general question from the approved answer library only. If no approved answer fits, do not improvise: go to T08. |
| T05 | Give the status of a case to a verified caller, read from the case system exactly as recorded, with the next step the procedure names. |
| T06 | Guide the caller to the right service: which procedure applies, the documents needed, and the channel to use (portal, counter or phone). |
| T07 | Log the call: category, a short summary, what was said, and the outcome code (section 12, R7). |

### Step 3. Hand-off (Agent and Licensing Case Officer)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T08 | Transfer the call or open a callback request when the question is outside the approved answers, needs action on a case, the caller asks for a person, or the caller is distressed. |
| T09 | Call the caller back, answer the question or take the request into the licensing procedure, and log the result. |

### Step 4. Written follow-up (Agent drafts, Team Leader releases)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T10 | When the caller wants it in writing, draft a follow-up message that repeats only what was said on the call: case number, document list, next step and channel. |
| T11 | Check the draft against the case record and the approved answers, then release it to the registered mobile number or email address on the record. |

### Step 5. Complaints (Agent and Team Leader)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T12 | Record a complaint in the caller's words, give the complaint number, and tell the caller when they will hear back. |
| T13 | Accept the complaint, assign it, make sure the caller receives a reply with reasons, and close it. |

### Step 6. Close and assure

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T14 | Each day, review every call that ended in a transfer, callback or complaint and confirm each has an owner. | Team Leader |
| T15 | Each month, sample 5% of calls and check them against this SOP. Report any call that deviated. | Quality and Knowledge Analyst |
| T16 | Each month, update the approved answer library from changes to the licensing SOPs and from questions that had no approved answer. | Quality and Knowledge Analyst |

## 6. Call categories and routing

| Category | Examples | Handled by | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 General information | Fees, documents, opening hours, how a procedure works | Agent, from the approved answer library | Answered on the call |
| C2 Case status | "Where is my renewal?", "Why is it on hold?" | Agent, after identity verification | Status read from the case system |
| C3 Service navigation | "I want to open a café; what do I need?" | Agent | Procedure, documents and channel named |
| C4 Action on a case | A change, a waiver, an exception, a disputed decision | Licensing Case Officer, by callback | Request taken into the licensing procedure |
| C5 Complaint | About service, delay or staff | Team Leader | Complaint number and reply within 5 working days |
| C6 Out of scope | Another authority's service | Agent | Caller told which body handles it; no transfer outside Marsa |
| C7 Urgent safety or distress | A danger to someone, a caller in distress | Agent, then Team Leader at once | Caller told to call the emergency number if anyone is in danger; call passed to the Team Leader |

## 7. Controls

1. The contact centre never decides a licence, a permit, a fee, a waiver or an exception, and never promises an outcome.
2. Spoken answers come only from the approved answer library or the case record. Anything else is handed off (T08).
3. Case details are given only after identity verification (T03).
4. Every written message to a caller is released by the Team Leader before it is sent (rule R5).
5. Written messages go only to the registered mobile number or email address on the record, never to a number or address given on the call.
6. The caller is told at the start that an automated assistant may answer, and can ask for a person at any time.
7. Every call and every action is written to the audit log with who, what and when.
8. Recordings and caller data are used only to serve the caller, for quality checks and for the audit record.

## 8. Exceptions and escalation

| Situation | Handled by | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| No approved answer fits the question | Agent | Licensing Case Officer (callback) |
| Caller asks for a person | Agent | Team Leader or Case Officer, at once |
| Identity cannot be verified | Agent | None: give general information only |
| Caller disputes a licensing decision | Case Officer | Appeals (SOP-LIC-021) |
| Caller is abusive | Agent | Team Leader |
| Danger to someone, or a caller in distress | Agent | Team Leader at once; emergency number for the caller |
| Suspected fraud or impersonation | Agent | Compliance, immediately |
| An approved answer appears wrong | Agent | Quality and Knowledge Analyst, same day |

## 9. Records

Call log, recording, follow-up message drafts and released messages, callback requests, complaints, daily review, monthly sample report, and the approved answer library with its change history. Kept for 2 years; complaints for 7 years (synthetic retention periods).

## 10. Information a caller gives and how to reach the Authority

General information (C1, C3) needs no identity check. Case-specific questions (C2, C4) need D1 to D3. A caller may speak in Arabic or English, or in Hindi or Urdu through the language line.

### D1. Case or licence number
- **What it is:** the case number given when the case was opened, or the trade licence number.
- **What must be true:** it exists in the case system.

### D2. Caller's full name
- **What must be true:** it matches the licence holder, or an authorised representative on the case.

### D3. Verification answer
- **What it is:** the last four digits of the registered mobile number, and the month the licence expires.
- **What must be true:** both match the record. Two failed attempts end the identity check for that call.

### D4. Representative on file
- **What it is:** an authorisation letter already on the case under SOP-LIC-014 (document D5 there).
- **When needed:** when the caller is not the licence holder.
- **Note:** an authorisation cannot be given over the phone.

### SUB. How to reach the Authority
- **Phone:** the licensing and permits line, Sunday to Thursday 7:30 to 20:00, Friday 7:30 to 12:00.
- **Portal:** for applications, document uploads and case status at any time.
- **Counter:** for originals, changes to the registered email address or mobile number, and representatives.
- **Callback:** requested on the call; made within 1 working day to the registered number.

### VAL. When a verification is still valid
A caller verified on a call stays verified for that call only. A callback is made to the registered number, so it needs only D2 again. A new call needs D1 to D3 again.

## 11. Detailed description of each task

Each entry gives the job profile, when the task starts, what goes in, the steps, what comes out, and where a problem goes.

### T01. Answer the call
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** a call arrives on the line.
- **Steps:** greet the caller; say that this is the Marsa licensing line, that an automated assistant may answer, and that the call is recorded; offer Arabic or English, and the language line for Hindi or Urdu; say the caller can ask for a person at any time.
- **Output:** a call in progress in the caller's chosen language.
- **Time target:** 80% of calls answered within 30 seconds.

### T02. Find out what the caller needs
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the language is set.
- **Steps:** ask what the call is about; repeat it back in one sentence; choose one category from section 6. If the call covers two matters, handle them one at a time.
- **Output:** a call category.
- **Problems:** a C7 call (danger or distress) goes to the Team Leader at once; if anyone is in danger, tell the caller to call the emergency number first.

### T03. Verify the caller's identity
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the call is C2 or C4.
- **Inputs:** D1, D2, D3, and D4 for a representative.
- **Steps:** find the case or licence by D1; check the name (D2) against the holder or a representative on file; ask the verification answer (D3); record the result.
- **Output:** identity verified, or not verified with the reason.
- **Problems:** after two failed attempts, give general information only and tell the caller to use the portal or the counter. A caller who seems to be impersonating the holder is reported to Compliance (section 8).

### T04. Answer a general question
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the call is C1, or a C3 call needs facts.
- **Inputs:** the approved answer library.
- **Steps:** find the approved answer; give it in the caller's language without adding to it; give fees and deadlines exactly as written.
- **Output:** the question answered.
- **Problems:** no approved answer fits, or the caller's situation differs from the answer: say so and go to T08. Never guess a fee, a deadline or a rule.

### T05. Give the status of a case
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** identity is verified for a C2 call.
- **Inputs:** the case record.
- **Steps:** read the status and the next step exactly as recorded. For "on hold", read the list of missing items. Explain what the status means using SOP-LIC-014, rule R7.
- **Output:** the caller knows the status and the next step.
- **Problems:** the caller says the record is wrong: open a callback (T08) to the Case Officer; do not change the record.

### T06. Guide the caller to the right service
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the call is C3.
- **Steps:** ask what the caller wants to achieve; name the procedure that applies; give its document list and the channel to use, from the approved answers; offer a written follow-up (T10).
- **Output:** the caller knows which service, which documents and which channel.
- **Problems:** a service of another authority is C6: name the body; do not transfer outside Marsa.

### T07. Log the call
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the caller's matter is answered, handed off or recorded as a complaint.
- **Steps:** record the category, a short summary, what was said, any promise of a callback, and the outcome code (R7).
- **Output:** a complete call log entry.

### T08. Transfer or open a callback
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** no approved answer fits, the call needs action on a case (C4), the caller asks for a person, or the caller is distressed.
- **Steps:** tell the caller who will handle it and when; transfer if a Case Officer or the Team Leader is free; otherwise open a callback request with the call summary, so the caller does not have to repeat it.
- **Output:** a transferred call or a callback request with an owner.
- **Time target:** callback within 1 working day.

### T09. Call the caller back
- **Job profile:** Licensing Case Officer.
- **Starts when:** a callback request is assigned.
- **Steps:** call the registered number; confirm the name (VAL); answer, or take the request into the licensing procedure and give the caller the case number; log the result.
- **Output:** a callback made and logged.
- **Problems:** no answer after two attempts on different days: send a released follow-up (T10, T11) asking the caller to call the line.

### T10. Draft a written follow-up
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the caller asks for the information in writing, or T09 ends with no answer.
- **Steps:** draft a message that repeats only what was said or recorded: case number, document list, next step, channel and timing. Write in the caller's language.
- **Output:** a draft waiting for release.
- **Problems:** a draft never states a decision, a waiver or a deadline that is not on the record.

### T11. Release the written follow-up
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Team Leader.
- **Starts when:** a draft is waiting.
- **Steps:** check every fact against the case record and the approved answers (control 2); correct or reject the draft; send it to the registered mobile number or email address on the record (control 5).
- **Output:** a released message recorded on the case.
- **Time target:** within 2 business hours of the call.

### T12. Record a complaint
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Agent.
- **Starts when:** the caller makes a complaint (C5).
- **Steps:** record the complaint in the caller's words; give the complaint number; tell the caller they will receive a reply within 5 working days.
- **Output:** a complaint recorded and acknowledged on the call.

### T13. Handle and close the complaint
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Team Leader.
- **Starts when:** a complaint is recorded.
- **Steps:** accept it; assign it to the team concerned; make sure the reply gives reasons and any action taken; release the reply (as T11); close the complaint.
- **Output:** a closed complaint with a reply sent.
- **Problems:** a complaint about a licensing decision is an appeal matter and goes to SOP-LIC-021.

### T14. Daily review of hand-offs
- **Job profile:** Contact Centre Team Leader.
- **Starts when:** each working day ends.
- **Steps:** list every call that ended in a transfer, callback or complaint; confirm each has an owner and a due time; chase any that are late.
- **Output:** the daily hand-off list with owners.

### T15. Monthly quality sample
- **Job profile:** Quality and Knowledge Analyst.
- **Starts when:** each month ends.
- **Steps:** sample 5% of calls; check the disclosure (control 6), the identity check, answers against the library, and the log; report any call that deviated.
- **Output:** the monthly sample report.

### T16. Update the approved answer library
- **Job profile:** Quality and Knowledge Analyst.
- **Starts when:** each month ends, or a licensing SOP changes.
- **Steps:** collect questions with no approved answer and changes in the licensing SOPs; draft new or changed answers; get the process owner's approval of the wording; publish them and retire answers that no longer apply.
- **Output:** an updated library with its change history.

## 12. Decision rules for the contact centre

### R1. Who decides
The contact centre decides nothing about a licence, a permit, a fee, a waiver or an exception. Those decisions are made only under the licensing procedure that governs them.

### R2. Answer on the call when
The question is C1, C2 or C3; an approved answer or the case record covers it fully; and, for C2, the caller is verified.

### R3. Hand off when
No approved answer fits; the call needs action on a case; the caller asks for a person; the caller is distressed; or the caller disputes a decision.

### R4. Identity
Case details are given only to a verified caller (T03). Two failed attempts end the identity check for that call.

### R5. Written messages
Every written message to a caller is drafted from the call or the record and released by the Team Leader. It goes only to the registered contact on the record.

### R6. Out of scope
A matter for another authority is named, not transferred. A matter for another Marsa department is transferred inside Marsa.

### R7. Call outcome codes
"Resolved": answered on the call. "Transferred": passed to a person during the call. "Callback": a callback request is open. "Complaint": a complaint is recorded. "Redirected": out of scope, caller told where to go.

## 13. Questions callers ask, and the answer under this SOP

### Q1. Am I speaking to a person?
An automated assistant may answer. You can ask for a person at any time and you will be transferred, or called back within 1 working day.

### Q2. What is the status of my renewal?
After we verify your identity with your case or licence number, your name and two details from your record, we read you the status and the next step.

### Q3. Can you waive my late fee?
No one on this line can. A waiver request is decided by the Licensing Supervisor. We can take your request and pass it on through a callback.

### Q4. Can you send me the document list by text?
Yes. A team leader checks it and sends it to the mobile number or email address registered on your licence within 2 business hours.

### Q5. Can you send it to a different number or email?
No. We send only to the contact registered on your licence. You can change it at the counter with the owner's identity document.

### Q6. Can I give someone permission to call for me?
Not by phone. The licence owner must submit an authorisation letter on the case, through the portal or at the counter.

### Q7. How do I make a complaint?
Tell us on the call. You get a complaint number at once and a reply with reasons within 5 working days.

### Q8. When is the line open?
Sunday to Thursday 7:30 to 20:00, and Friday 7:30 to 12:00. The portal is open at all times.
