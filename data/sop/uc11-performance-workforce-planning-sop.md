# SOP-PRF-004: Annual performance review and workforce planning

> **Fictitious document.** Sahel Federal Workforce Agency, its rating scale, increments, systems and staff titles are invented for demonstration. Nothing here describes a real authority or real law.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document id | SOP-PRF-004 |
| Version | 1.0 (synthetic) |
| Owner | Head of Workforce Planning, Sahel Federal Workforce Agency |
| Related use case | UC11: Performance review and workforce planning support |
| Applies to | The annual performance cycle for employees at grades G4 to G10 (objectives, year-end review, calibration, rating, increment) and the annual workforce plan built from aggregate data |
| Review cycle | Every 12 months |

## 1. Purpose

Rate every employee fairly, on evidence against objectives agreed at the start of the year, and link pay increments to ratings by a published table. Build the workforce plan from aggregate data so that headcount and skills decisions are planned, not improvised. Analysts compile evidence and check fairness; only people rate people. No rating is final until the Calibration Panel Chair confirms it, and no workforce plan is used to select individuals.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** objective setting; year-end self-assessment and manager review; evidence packs; calibration; final ratings; increment calculation; the review conversation and rating notice; the annual workforce plan (headcount, skills and succession, at department and grade level).

**Out of scope:** probation reviews (SOP-PRB-002); disciplinary matters (SOP-ER-005); rating appeals (SOP-PRF-008); restructuring and redundancy (SOP-WFP-006); creating or funding positions (SOP-WFP-002); recruitment (SOP-REC-011).

## 3. Roles

Five job profiles carry out this procedure. Each has a distinct purpose, a fixed set of tasks and its own limits.

### 3.1 Line Manager (Rater)
- **Purpose:** agree objectives with the employee and propose a rating on evidence.
- **Works with:** the employee, the HR Business Partner, the Calibration Panel.
- **Authority:** may agree objectives, propose a rating with written evidence, and hold the review conversation. May not finalise a rating.
- **Skills:** evidence-based assessment, the rating scale (section 6.1), feedback conversations.

### 3.2 Workforce Planning Analyst
- **Purpose:** compile evidence packs, check rating fairness, calculate increments and build the workforce plan from aggregate data.
- **Works with:** the HR Business Partner, the Calibration Panel Chair, the Head of Workforce Planning.
- **Authority:** may read the Performance System and HR data, produce evidence packs, fairness reports, increment calculations and plan scenarios. May not propose or change an individual rating, or name individuals in a workforce plan.
- **Skills:** HR data, statistics, the fairness checks (section 6.3), scenario modelling.

### 3.3 HR Business Partner
- **Purpose:** keep the cycle complete, consistent and fair in their departments.
- **Works with:** line managers, the Analyst, the Panel Chair.
- **Authority:** may check objectives and proposals for completeness, return a proposal without evidence, and sit on the panel without a vote.
- **Skills:** performance policy, coaching managers.

### 3.4 Calibration Panel Chair
- **Purpose:** lead calibration and finalise ratings.
- **Works with:** two senior managers on the panel, the HR Business Partner, the Analyst.
- **Authority:** the only role that may finalise or change a rating. Every change is recorded with reasons.
- **Skills:** consistent judgement across teams, the rating scale, recorded reasoning.

### 3.5 Head of Workforce Planning (Approving Officer for the plan)
- **Purpose:** approve the workforce plan.
- **Works with:** the Analyst, department heads, the Director of Strategy.
- **Authority:** the only role that may approve the workforce plan and its assumptions.
- **Skills:** strategic workforce planning, budget alignment.

## 4. Procedure

Task ids are used in the audit log and the training material.

### Step 1. Objectives

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T01 | Agree 3 to 6 objectives with each employee by 31 January and record them in the Performance System. | Line Manager |
| T02 | Check every employee has recorded objectives that are specific and measurable; return any that are not. | HR Business Partner |

### Step 2. Year-end review

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T03 | Compile each employee's evidence pack: objectives, results recorded, mid-year notes, self-assessment and approved leave periods. The pack contains no proposed rating. | Workforce Planning Analyst |
| T04 | Propose a rating with written evidence for each objective. | Line Manager |
| T05 | Return any proposal that lacks evidence for each objective. | HR Business Partner |

### Step 3. Calibration

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T06 | Produce the distribution and fairness report for each department. | Workforce Planning Analyst |
| T07 | Calibrate proposals across teams and finalise each rating, recording the reason for any change. | Calibration Panel Chair |

### Step 4. Outcomes

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T08 | Calculate each increment from the final rating using the table. | Workforce Planning Analyst |
| T09 | Hold the review conversation and give the employee the written rating notice with the evidence, the increment and the appeal route. | Line Manager |

### Step 5. Workforce plan

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T10 | Build the baseline: headcount, vacancies, expected leavers and retirements, and skills coverage by department and grade, from aggregate data only. | Workforce Planning Analyst |
| T11 | Model demand scenarios from approved strategy and budget assumptions, and show the gaps. | Workforce Planning Analyst |
| T12 | Consult department heads on the scenarios and record their comments. | HR Business Partner |
| T13 | Approve the workforce plan and its assumptions. | Head of Workforce Planning |

### Step 6. Assure

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T14 | Sample 10% of final ratings and check each against the evidence and this SOP. | HR Business Partner |
| T15 | After each cycle, review the fairness report across the agency and report to the Head of Workforce Planning. | Workforce Planning Analyst |

## 6. Rating scale, increments and fairness checks (fictitious)

### 6.1 Rating scale and increments

| Rating | Meaning | Increment |
|---|---|---|
| 5 Outstanding | Exceeded all objectives with exceptional impact | 2 steps |
| 4 Exceeds | Exceeded most objectives | 1.5 steps |
| 3 Meets | Met objectives | 1 step |
| 2 Partly meets | Met some objectives | 0.5 step and a development plan |
| 1 Does not meet | Did not meet most objectives | No increment; improvement plan under SOP-PRB-004 |

A step is the grade's annual step in SOP-REC-011, section 6. Example: G6 (step 500) rated 4 is 1.5 × 500, an increment of 750. The increment is capped at the top of the band. Amounts are in a synthetic currency unit.

### 6.2 No forced distribution
There is no quota for any rating. The distribution is checked only to find inconsistency between teams.

### 6.3 Fairness checks
The Analyst flags, for the Panel:
1. A team whose average proposed rating differs from the department average by more than 0.5.
2. A difference of more than 0.3 in average rating between groups by gender, nationality, part-time status, or approved long leave.
3. Any employee rated 1 or 2 whose evidence pack shows no mid-year note.
4. Any employee on maternity or other approved long leave whose objectives were not adjusted for the time worked.

## 7. Controls

1. Ratings are proposed only by line managers and finalised only by the Calibration Panel Chair.
2. The Analyst never proposes or changes an individual rating; evidence packs contain no rating.
3. Every rating has written evidence per objective; every change at calibration has a recorded reason.
4. Approved leave never lowers a rating; objectives are judged on time worked.
5. Increments come only from the table.
6. The workforce plan uses aggregate data and names no individual.
7. Performance data is used only for this cycle, the appeal and the audit record.
8. Every action is written to the audit log with who, what and when.

## 8. Exceptions and escalation

| Situation | Handled by | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| Fairness flag not explained at calibration | Panel Chair | Head of Workforce Planning |
| Manager has a conflict of interest | HR Business Partner | Next-level manager rates |
| Employee disputes the rating | Line Manager | Appeals (SOP-PRF-008) |
| Plan scenario implies post reductions | Head of Workforce Planning | Director of Strategy; SOP-WFP-006 |
| Request to use plan data about individuals | Analyst | Data Protection Officer |

## 9. Records

Objectives, evidence packs, proposals, fairness reports, calibration records with reasons, final ratings, increment calculations, rating notices, workforce plan and its approval, sample reports. Kept for 7 years after employment ends (synthetic retention period).

## 10. Required documents and how to submit

### D1. Objectives record
- **What must be true:** 3 to 6 objectives, each with a measure and a due date, agreed by the employee and manager.

### D2. Self-assessment
- **What must be true:** the employee's account of results against each objective, submitted in the Performance System.

### D3. Mid-year note
- **What it is:** the manager's record of the mid-year conversation.

### D4. Manager's proposal
- **What must be true:** a proposed rating and evidence for each objective.

### D5. Strategy and budget assumptions
- **What it is:** the approved strategy priorities and budget envelope for the plan year, from the Director of Strategy.

### SUB. How to submit
All of D1 to D4 are entered in the Performance System; paper or email submissions are not accepted. D5 is sent by the Director of Strategy to the Head of Workforce Planning.

### VAL. When a record stays valid
Objectives may be changed by agreement up to 30 September; after that only for approved long leave or a change of job.

## 11. Detailed description of each task

### T01. Agree objectives
- **Job profile:** Line Manager.
- **Output:** D1 recorded for every employee by 31 January.

### T02. Check objectives
- **Job profile:** HR Business Partner.
- **Steps:** check each employee has 3 to 6 objectives with measures and dates; return any that do not.
- **Output:** a completeness report per department.

### T03. Compile the evidence pack
- **Job profile:** Workforce Planning Analyst.
- **Steps:** gather D1 to D3, recorded results, training completed and approved leave periods; summarise factually; add no rating or opinion.
- **Output:** one evidence pack per employee.

### T04. Propose a rating
- **Job profile:** Line Manager.
- **Steps:** read the pack; rate against each objective; write the evidence; propose an overall rating.
- **Output:** D4.

### T05. Check proposals
- **Job profile:** HR Business Partner.
- **Output:** proposals accepted for calibration, or returned for missing evidence.

### T06. Fairness report
- **Job profile:** Workforce Planning Analyst.
- **Steps:** compute the distribution by team; apply the checks in section 6.3; list each flag with the data behind it. Group data is shown only where the group has at least 5 people.
- **Output:** a fairness report per department.

### T07. Calibrate and finalise
- **Job profile:** Calibration Panel Chair.
- **Steps:** review proposals and flags; ask managers to explain flagged differences; confirm or change each rating, recording the reason for a change.
- **Output:** final ratings.

### T08. Calculate increments
- **Job profile:** Workforce Planning Analyst.
- **Steps:** apply section 6.1 to each final rating and the employee's grade step; cap at the band top.
- **Output:** an increment list, checked by a second analyst.

### T09. Review conversation and notice
- **Job profile:** Line Manager.
- **Steps:** meet the employee; explain the rating and evidence; give the written notice with the increment and the right to appeal within 15 working days under SOP-PRF-008.
- **Output:** a notice given and recorded.

### T10. Build the baseline
- **Job profile:** Workforce Planning Analyst.
- **Steps:** count headcount and vacancies by department and grade; estimate leavers from 3-year averages and known retirements; map skills coverage from the skills register.
- **Output:** the baseline, with no individual named.

### T11. Model scenarios
- **Job profile:** Workforce Planning Analyst.
- **Steps:** apply D5 to project demand; show for each scenario the gap by department, grade and skill, and the cost.
- **Output:** two or three scenarios with assumptions stated.

### T12. Consult department heads
- **Job profile:** HR Business Partner.
- **Output:** recorded comments per department.

### T13. Approve the plan
- **Job profile:** Head of Workforce Planning.
- **Output:** an approved plan, sent to the Director of Strategy.

### T14. Sample ratings
- **Job profile:** HR Business Partner.
- **Output:** a sample report listing any rating without matching evidence.

### T15. Agency fairness review
- **Job profile:** Workforce Planning Analyst.
- **Output:** an agency-wide fairness report with trends over three cycles.

## 12. Decision rules

### R1. Who decides
Line managers propose; only the Calibration Panel Chair finalises a rating; only the Head of Workforce Planning approves the plan. Analysis informs; it never decides.

### R2. Evidence
A rating without evidence for each objective is not finalised.

### R3. Leave
Approved leave never lowers a rating.

### R4. Increments
Increments follow section 6.1 exactly.

### R5. Fairness flags
Every flag is explained at calibration or escalated.

### R6. Aggregate planning
The workforce plan works at department, grade and skill level and is never used to choose individuals.

### R7. Statuses
"Objectives set", "Self-assessed", "Proposed", "Final", "Notified", "Under appeal".

## 13. Questions employees ask, and the answer under this SOP

### Q1. Who decides my rating?
Your manager proposes it with evidence; the Calibration Panel Chair finalises it.

### Q2. Is there a quota for top ratings?
No (section 6.2).

### Q3. I was on maternity leave. Will that lower my rating?
No. You are assessed on objectives adjusted to the time you worked (R3).

### Q4. How much will my increment be?
Your rating's steps from section 6.1 times your grade's step, capped at the top of your band.

### Q5. I disagree with my rating.
You may appeal within 15 working days of the notice under SOP-PRF-008.
