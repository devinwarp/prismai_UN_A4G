# SOP RA-02: Monthly archive of internal inspection records

Marsa Public Services Authority (fictitious). Records office. Synthetic document for demonstration only. Staff-only task: no public contact.

- The records clerk collects the scanned inspection reports from the shared mailbox at month end.
- The clerk checks each scan for readable pages and a report number.
- The clerk renames each file to the standard pattern and copies it into the Marsa Records Workbook.
- The clerk matches every report number against the Marsa Inspection Register and notes any gap (about 20 minutes per batch).
- The records officer reviews the list of gaps and decides which ones need a follow-up note.
- The clerk logs the batch in the monthly tracker and updates the index.
- The clerk moves the finished batch to the archive folder and closes the task.
