# SOP-HRS-002: Employee HR policy enquiries (self-service)

> **Fictitious document.** Sahel Federal Workforce Agency, its policies, leave entitlements, allowances, systems and staff titles are invented for demonstration. Nothing here describes a real authority or real law.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document id | SOP-HRS-002 |
| Version | 1.0 (synthetic) |
| Owner | Head of HR Shared Services, Sahel Federal Workforce Agency |
| Related use case | UC10: Employee self-service HR assistant |
| Applies to | Answering Sahel employees' questions about HR policies (leave, allowances, working hours, benefits, conduct, learning), and keeping the HR Policy Library those answers come from |
| Review cycle | Every 12 months |

## 1. Purpose

Give every employee a quick, correct and consistent answer about what Sahel's HR policies say, cited to the policy so they can check it. The enquiry service explains policy; it does not decide anything about an employee, read or change their personal record, or submit requests for them. Sensitive matters (grievances, harassment, wellbeing, disciplinary cases) always go to a person, confidentially.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** questions from Sahel employees about Published HR policies; directing employees to the right self-service form or HR contact; the HR Policy Library (adding, reviewing, publishing and retiring policies); gaps and conflicts.

**Out of scope:** an employee's own balances, payslips or records (shown in the HR Self-Service System); submitting or approving any request (leave, allowance, transfer); individual decisions or exceptions (the HR Business Partner); grievances and disciplinary matters (SOP-ER-005); recruitment (SOP-REC-011); performance ratings (SOP-PRF-004).

## 3. Roles

Five job profiles carry out this procedure. Each has a distinct purpose, a fixed set of tasks and its own limits.

### 3.1 HR Policy Information Officer
- **Purpose:** answer employees' policy questions from the HR Policy Library, citing each policy.
- **Works with:** Sahel employees (intranet chat and email), the HR Shared Services Supervisor.
- **Authority:** may search the library, answer from Published policies and name the self-service form or contact. May not read or change an employee's record, submit a request, promise an outcome or grant an exception.
- **Skills:** reading policy precisely, plain writing in Arabic and English, the sensitive-topic screen (section 6.3).

### 3.2 HR Policy Officer
- **Purpose:** keep the HR Policy Library complete, current and consistent.
- **Works with:** policy owners, the Employment Law Adviser, the Head of HR Shared Services.
- **Authority:** may prepare policies for publication, record metadata and answer escalated questions after review. May not publish.
- **Skills:** document control, HR policy structure.

### 3.3 Employment Law Adviser
- **Purpose:** confirm that policies and escalated answers are consistent with federal employment rules.
- **Works with:** the HR Policy Officer.
- **Authority:** may approve or reject a policy text or escalated answer for legal consistency.
- **Skills:** federal employment law, HR casework.

### 3.4 HR Shared Services Supervisor
- **Purpose:** own the quality of answers and handle escalations.
- **Works with:** the HR Policy Information Officer, HR Business Partners, the HR Policy Officer.
- **Authority:** may answer an escalated question, close an escalation and assign a matter to an HR Business Partner. May not publish or change a policy.
- **Skills:** HR operations, judgement on sensitive matters.

### 3.5 Head of HR Shared Services (Approving Officer)
- **Purpose:** decide what is in the HR Policy Library.
- **Works with:** all four roles above, policy owners.
- **Authority:** the only role that may publish, withdraw or retire a policy and resolve a conflict between policies.
- **Skills:** HR policy, federal employment framework.

## 4. Procedure

Task ids are used in the audit log and the training material.

### Step 1. Answer a question (HR Policy Information Officer)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T01 | Start with the AI disclosure and the language choice, and say that the service explains policy but cannot see or change personal records. |
| T02 | Run the sensitive-topic screen. If the question concerns harassment, a grievance, a disciplinary matter, wellbeing or safety, offer a confidential HR Business Partner contact at once and do not search further. |
| T03 | Search Published policies in force; answer in plain words and cite each policy section. Quote entitlements, amounts and periods exactly. |
| T04 | If the employee asks for their own balance or record, or to submit a request, name the self-service form or system screen; take no action. |
| T05 | If no policy covers the question, policies conflict, or the answer depends on the employee's individual circumstances, say so and escalate to the HR Shared Services Supervisor. |

### Step 2. Escalations (HR Shared Services Supervisor)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T06 | Answer the escalated question, or assign it to an HR Business Partner, within 2 working days. |
| T07 | Log each gap or conflict for the HR Policy Officer. |

### Step 3. Maintain the library

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T08 | Receive a new or revised policy and record its metadata (id, version, effective date, owner, grades covered, what it supersedes). | HR Policy Officer |
| T09 | Review it for consistency with federal employment rules. | Employment Law Adviser |
| T10 | Approve or refuse publication. | Head of HR Shared Services |
| T11 | Publish on the effective date and mark replaced versions "Superseded". | HR Policy Officer |

### Step 4. Assure

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T12 | Each week, check a 5% sample of answers for accuracy, citation and tone. | HR Shared Services Supervisor |
| T13 | Each quarter, review the gap log and policies past their review date, and report to the Head of HR Shared Services. | HR Policy Officer |

### 6.3 Sensitive-topic screen
Harassment or bullying; discrimination; grievance against a manager or colleague; disciplinary action; personal safety; distress or wellbeing concerns; whistleblowing. Reply: a confidential HR Business Partner contact (hrbp@sahel.example, extension 4410) and, for distress, the Employee Assistance Programme line.

### 6.4 Citation format
`[id] v[version], section [number]`. Every entitlement or rule in an answer carries a citation.

## 7. Controls

1. Every conversation starts with the AI disclosure.
2. The sensitive-topic screen runs before every answer; those topics always go to a person.
3. Answers come only from Published policies in force, each statement cited, numbers quoted exactly.
4. The service cannot read or change personal records and does not submit requests.
5. Questions that depend on an employee's individual circumstances are escalated, not decided.
6. Only the Head of HR Shared Services publishes, withdraws or retires a policy.
7. Questions are logged without employee identifiers beyond the employee number; sensitive-topic conversations are not stored after referral.
8. Every library change and answer is logged with who, what and when.

## 8. Exceptions and escalation

| Situation | Handled by | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| Sensitive topic | Information Officer | HR Business Partner, confidentially |
| Employee in distress or danger | Information Officer | Employee Assistance Programme; emergency number if danger |
| No policy covers the question | Information Officer | HR Shared Services Supervisor |
| Policies conflict | Supervisor | Head of HR Shared Services |
| Employee asks for an exception | Information Officer | HR Business Partner |
| Policy appears to break federal rules | Any role | Employment Law Adviser, at once |

## 9. Records

Answer log with citations, escalation log, gap log, policy versions and approvals, weekly sample, quarterly review. Answer logs kept for 1 year; policy records for 10 years (synthetic retention periods).

## 10. Required documents for a policy and how to submit

### D1. Approved policy text
- **What must be true:** final text approved by the policy owner, with id, version, effective date and review date.

### D2. Metadata sheet
- **What it is:** id, title, version, effective date, owner, grades covered, ids superseded.

### D3. Legal consistency record
- **What must be true:** signed by the Employment Law Adviser.

### D4. Parallel translation
- **What must be true:** Arabic and English say the same; the Arabic text governs if they differ.

### SUB. How to submit
Policy owners send D1 and D2 to the HR Policy Officer through the policy mailbox at least 10 working days before the effective date.

### VAL. When a policy stays valid
A Published policy stays in force until superseded or withdrawn. A policy past its review date stays in force and is listed in the quarterly review.

## 11. Detailed description of each task

### T01. Disclose and set the language
- **Job profile:** HR Policy Information Officer.
- **Output:** a conversation with the disclosure and language recorded.

### T02. Sensitive-topic screen
- **Job profile:** HR Policy Information Officer.
- **Steps:** check the question against section 6.3 before any search. If it matches, reply with the confidential contact only, and do not ask for details.
- **Output:** a referral, or the question passed on.

### T03. Answer with citations
- **Job profile:** HR Policy Information Officer.
- **Steps:** search Published policies; find the rule for the employee's stated grade if it matters; answer in plain words; cite each statement. Example: a G8 employee asking about annual leave is told 30 working days a year, up to 10 days carried over (HRP-LV-01 v1, section 3).
- **Output:** a cited answer.
- **Problems:** if the grade matters and the employee has not given it, give each grade's rule rather than guess.

### T04. Redirect personal-record and request questions
- **Job profile:** HR Policy Information Officer.
- **Steps:** name the screen in the HR Self-Service System (for example "My leave > Balance" or "Requests > New leave request"); take no action.
- **Output:** a reply with the right screen.

### T05. Abstain and escalate
- **Job profile:** HR Policy Information Officer.
- **Starts when:** no policy covers the question, policies conflict, or the answer depends on individual circumstances (for example "Can my manager refuse my leave?").
- **Steps:** state what the policy does say, cited; say what cannot be answered; escalate.
- **Output:** a partial answer and an escalation.

### T06. Answer an escalation
- **Job profile:** HR Shared Services Supervisor.
- **Output:** an answer to the employee, or an assignment to an HR Business Partner.

### T07. Log the gap or conflict
- **Job profile:** HR Shared Services Supervisor.
- **Output:** an entry in the gap log.

### T08. Receive and record a policy
- **Job profile:** HR Policy Officer.
- **Output:** a Draft entry with metadata and a note of conflicts with existing policies.

### T09. Legal consistency review
- **Job profile:** Employment Law Adviser.
- **Output:** D3, approved or rejected with reasons.

### T10. Approve publication
- **Job profile:** Head of HR Shared Services.
- **Output:** a recorded decision.

### T11. Publish
- **Job profile:** HR Policy Officer.
- **Steps:** publish on the effective date; mark replaced versions Superseded; rebuild the search index; announce the change on the intranet.
- **Output:** the library updated and logged.

### T12. Weekly sample
- **Job profile:** HR Shared Services Supervisor.
- **Steps:** check 5% of answers: disclosure, screen applied, every statement cited, numbers exact, no decision or record access.
- **Output:** the weekly sample report.

### T13. Quarterly review
- **Job profile:** HR Policy Officer.
- **Output:** a report of recurring gaps and past-review policies to the Head of HR Shared Services.

## 12. Decision rules for an answer

### R1. Who decides
The service decides nothing about an employee. Leave and allowance requests are decided by the line manager and HR under the relevant policy. Only the Head of HR Shared Services decides what is in the library.

### R2. Sensitive first
A question that matches the sensitive-topic screen goes to a person.

### R3. Source
Every statement comes from a Published policy in force and is cited.

### R4. No records, no requests
The service does not read, change or submit anything in an employee's record.

### R5. Individual circumstances
Where the answer depends on facts the policy leaves to a manager's or HR's judgement, the service gives the rule and escalates.

### R6. Language
If translations differ, the Arabic text governs and the difference is logged.

### R7. Statuses
Policies are Draft, Published, Superseded or Withdrawn.

## 13. Questions employees ask, and the answer under this SOP

### Q1. How much annual leave do I get?
25 working days a year for G4 to G7, 30 for G8 to G10, with up to 10 days carried over (HRP-LV-01).

### Q2. How many days do I have left?
The service cannot see your balance. Open "My leave > Balance" in the HR Self-Service System.

### Q3. Can you book my leave for next week?
No. Submit a request under "Requests > New leave request"; your manager approves it.

### Q4. My manager keeps shouting at me. What can I do?
Please contact an HR Business Partner confidentially at hrbp@sahel.example or extension 4410.

### Q5. Do I need a sick note for one day off?
No. A certificate is needed for more than 2 consecutive days (HRP-LV-01).
