# SOP FW-03: Late-fee waiver and penalty decision

Marsa Public Services Authority (fictitious). Licensing and finance. Synthetic document for demonstration only.

## Intake

A citizen who was charged a late fee or a penalty can ask for a waiver. The intake clerk registers the request in the Marsa Case System and sends the applicant a receipt by email. The clerk attaches the original fee notice and any evidence the applicant supplied.

## Eligibility

The case officer checks the applicant's payment history in the Marsa Payment Ledger. The officer determines whether the applicant is eligible for a waiver under the published hardship rules. When the applicant has two or more earlier late fees, the case officer escalates the case to the review committee.

## Decision

The review committee decides whether to grant a full waiver, a partial waiver or no waiver. The committee chair signs off the decision. Where the committee imposes a penalty or suspends the licence, the decision is final and cannot be undone.

## Notice and appeal

The case officer sends the applicant a decision letter that explains the outcome and the right to appeal. If the applicant appeals, the case officer forwards the file to the appeals officer. The finance officer then releases any refund and updates the Marsa Payment Ledger.
