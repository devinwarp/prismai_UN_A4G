# SOP-CLG-004: Clinical policy and procedure enquiries for staff

> **Fictitious document.** Wahat Health Services Authority, its policies, thresholds, systems and staff titles are invented for demonstration. Nothing here is clinical guidance or describes a real authority or real law.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document id | SOP-CLG-004 |
| Version | 1.0 (synthetic) |
| Owner | Head of Clinical Governance, Wahat Health Services Authority |
| Related use case | UC07: Clinical policy and procedure assistant for staff |
| Applies to | Answering Wahat clinical and support staff's questions about approved clinical policies, procedures and the formulary, and keeping the Clinical Policy Library those answers come from |
| Review cycle | Every 12 months |

## 1. Purpose

Let every member of staff find out quickly what an approved Wahat policy says, with a citation they can open and check. The enquiry service tells staff what the policy says; it never makes a clinical judgement about a patient. Clinical decisions stay with the treating clinician, and urgent clinical situations always go to the escalation route in the policy, never to an enquiry.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** questions from Wahat staff about approved clinical policies, procedures, protocols and the Wahat Formulary; the Clinical Policy Library (adding, reviewing, publishing and retiring documents); gaps and conflicts between documents.

**Out of scope:** advice about an individual patient's diagnosis or treatment; dose calculation for a patient; questions from patients or the public (SOP-PHC-006 and SOP-APT-003); writing new clinical policy (SOP-CLG-001); incident reporting (SOP-CLG-010).

## 3. Roles

Four job profiles carry out this procedure. Each has a distinct purpose, a fixed set of tasks and its own limits.

### 3.1 Clinical Policy Information Officer
- **Purpose:** answer staff questions from the Clinical Policy Library, with a citation for every statement.
- **Works with:** Wahat staff (chat and intranet), the Clinical Governance Officer.
- **Authority:** may search the library and answer from Published documents. May not give advice on a specific patient, calculate a dose, answer from memory or drafts, or change the library.
- **Skills:** precise reading of clinical policy, the citation format, plain writing in Arabic and English.

### 3.2 Clinical Governance Officer
- **Purpose:** keep the Clinical Policy Library complete, current and consistent.
- **Works with:** policy owners, the Clinical Policy Reviewer, the Head of Clinical Governance.
- **Authority:** may prepare documents for publication, record metadata, mark review dates, and answer escalated questions after clinical review. May not publish.
- **Skills:** document control, version management, the policy framework.

### 3.3 Clinical Policy Reviewer
- **Purpose:** confirm that a document or an escalated answer is clinically correct. A senior clinician or, for medicines, a medication safety pharmacist.
- **Works with:** the Clinical Governance Officer, policy owners.
- **Authority:** may approve or reject a document for publication readiness and an escalated answer.
- **Skills:** the clinical specialty concerned, evidence appraisal.

### 3.4 Head of Clinical Governance (Approving Officer)
- **Purpose:** decide what is in the library.
- **Works with:** all three roles above, the Medical Director.
- **Authority:** the only role that may publish, withdraw or retire a document and resolve a conflict between documents.
- **Skills:** clinical governance, patient safety, judgement on conflicting guidance.

## 4. Procedure

Task ids are used in the audit log and the training material.

### Step 1. Maintain the library

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T01 | Receive a new or revised document from its owner, approved by the owning clinical committee. | Clinical Governance Officer |
| T02 | Record metadata (id, title, version, effective date, review date, owner, what it supersedes) and check for conflicts with other documents. | Clinical Governance Officer |
| T03 | Review the document for clinical accuracy and consistency with the formulary. | Clinical Policy Reviewer |
| T04 | Approve or refuse publication. | Head of Clinical Governance |
| T05 | Publish on the effective date; mark replaced documents "Superseded". | Clinical Governance Officer |

### Step 2. Answer a question (Clinical Policy Information Officer)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T06 | Screen the question. If it describes a patient who may be deteriorating or in danger, give the escalation route from CP-ESC-01 at once and nothing else. |
| T07 | Check the question asks what a policy says, not what to do for a named or described patient. |
| T08 | Search Published documents in force; answer in plain words with a citation for every statement; quote numbers, thresholds and doses exactly as written. |
| T09 | If no document covers the question, documents conflict, or the answer would need a calculation or a judgement, say so, cite what exists, and escalate. |

### Step 3. Gaps and conflicts

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T10 | Log the gap or conflict with the question and documents involved. | Clinical Policy Information Officer |
| T11 | Answer the escalated question after clinical review, and propose a library change if needed. | Clinical Governance Officer |
| T12 | Resolve a conflict between documents and record the ruling. | Head of Clinical Governance |

### Step 4. Assure

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T13 | Each week, check a 5% sample of answers, and every answer about medicines, for accuracy and citation. | Clinical Policy Reviewer |
| T14 | Each month, list documents past their review date and tell the owners and the Head of Clinical Governance. | Clinical Governance Officer |

## 5. Timings

| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Urgent-situation screen | Immediate, before any search |
| Answer from the library | Within 5 minutes, 24 hours a day |
| Escalated question answered | Within 2 working days (1 working day for medicines) |
| Conflict resolved | Within 5 working days; a safety conflict within 1 working day |

## 6. Library contents and citation format (fictitious)

### 6.1 Documents in the Clinical Policy Library

| Id | Title |
|---|---|
| CP-ESC-01 | Recognising and escalating the deteriorating adult patient |
| CP-IPC-01 | Hand hygiene |
| CP-IPC-04 | Isolation precautions |
| CP-MED-02 | Medicines administration and double-checking |
| CP-CON-03 | Consent to examination and treatment |
| CP-DOC-01 | Clinical record keeping |
| FORM-2026 | Wahat Formulary 2026 |

### 6.2 Escalation route (CP-ESC-01, section 4, synthetic)

| Early warning score | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 to 4 | Continue routine observations |
| 5 or 6, or 3 in any single parameter | Nurse in charge informs the responsible doctor; review within 30 minutes |
| 7 or more | Call the rapid response team on 2222 at once |

### 6.3 Citation format
`[id] v[version], section [number], effective [date]`. Every statement of policy in an answer carries one citation.

### 6.4 Document statuses
"Draft", "Published", "Superseded", "Withdrawn", and "Under review" (Published but past its review date; still in force and shown with a warning).

## 7. Controls

1. The urgent-situation screen (T06) runs before every answer.
2. Answers come only from Published documents in force; every statement is cited.
3. Doses, thresholds and timings are quoted exactly; nothing is calculated, converted or adjusted for a patient.
4. The service never advises on an individual patient; it states the policy and who decides.
5. No source, conflict, or a need for judgement means no answer on that point, and an escalation.
6. Only the Head of Clinical Governance publishes, withdraws or retires a document.
7. Staff do not enter patient identifiers; any that arrive are removed from the log.
8. Every question, answer and library change is logged with who, what and when.

## 8. Exceptions and escalation

| Situation | Handled by | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| Question describes a deteriorating or endangered patient | Information Officer | Escalation route CP-ESC-01, at once |
| No document covers the question | Information Officer | Clinical Governance Officer |
| Documents conflict | Information Officer | Head of Clinical Governance (T12) |
| Question about a medicine not in the formulary | Information Officer | Medication safety pharmacist |
| Staff member reports a document is unsafe | Any role | Head of Clinical Governance, at once, and SOP-CLG-010 |

## 9. Records

Question and answer log with citations, gap and conflict log, library change log, clinical review records, weekly sample, monthly review-date report. Questions and answers kept for 2 years; library records for 15 years (synthetic retention periods).

## 10. Required documents and how to submit

### D1. Approved document text
- **What must be true:** final text approved by the owning clinical committee, with id, version, effective date and review date.

### D2. Committee approval record
- **What must be true:** the minute or record of the committee approving D1.

### D3. Change summary
- **When needed:** any revision; states what changed clinically.

### D4. Formulary check
- **When needed:** any document that names a medicine or dose; signed by a pharmacist confirming consistency with FORM-2026.

### D5. Parallel translation
- **What must be true:** prepared by a registered medical translator; the English text governs if they differ.

### SUB. How to submit
Policy owners send D1 to D4 to the Clinical Governance Officer through the document control system at least 10 working days before the effective date.

### VAL. When a document stays valid
A Published document stays in force until superseded or withdrawn. After its review date it is "Under review": still in force, shown with a warning, and reported monthly (T14).

## 11. Detailed description of each task

### T01. Receive a document
- **Job profile:** Clinical Governance Officer.
- **Steps:** log the submission; check D1 to D4 are present; ask the owner for anything missing.
- **Output:** a logged submission.

### T02. Record metadata and check conflicts
- **Job profile:** Clinical Governance Officer.
- **Steps:** complete metadata; search the library for documents on the same topic; note any statement that differs.
- **Output:** a Draft entry with a conflict note.

### T03. Clinical review
- **Job profile:** Clinical Policy Reviewer.
- **Steps:** check accuracy, internal consistency and consistency with the formulary; approve or reject with reasons.
- **Output:** a recorded review.

### T04. Approve publication
- **Job profile:** Head of Clinical Governance.
- **Output:** a recorded approval or refusal.

### T05. Publish and retire
- **Job profile:** Clinical Governance Officer.
- **Steps:** set the document Published on its effective date; mark replaced documents Superseded; rebuild the search index.
- **Output:** the library updated and logged.

### T06. Urgent-situation screen
- **Job profile:** Clinical Policy Information Officer.
- **Steps:** before any search, check whether the question describes a patient who may be deteriorating, in danger, or with abnormal observations. If so, reply only with the escalation route in section 6.2 and the rapid response number, citing CP-ESC-01.
- **Output:** an escalation reply, or the question passed to T07.

### T07. Scope the question
- **Job profile:** Clinical Policy Information Officer.
- **Steps:** decide whether the question asks what a policy says (in scope) or what to do for a particular patient (out of scope). For the second, state the relevant policy with its citation and that the treating clinician decides.
- **Output:** a scoped question.

### T08. Answer with citations
- **Job profile:** Clinical Policy Information Officer.
- **Steps:** search Published documents in force; answer in plain words; cite every statement; quote numbers exactly; add a warning if the document is Under review.
- **Output:** a cited answer.

### T09. Abstain and escalate
- **Job profile:** Clinical Policy Information Officer.
- **Starts when:** no document covers the point, documents conflict, or the answer would need a calculation or judgement (for example a weight-based dose).
- **Steps:** say which part cannot be answered and why; cite what the documents do say; escalate.
- **Output:** a partial answer marking the gap, and an escalation.

### T10. Log the gap or conflict
- **Job profile:** Clinical Policy Information Officer.
- **Output:** an entry in the gap and conflict log.

### T11. Answer an escalated question
- **Job profile:** Clinical Governance Officer.
- **Steps:** prepare an answer from the documents; get the Clinical Policy Reviewer's approval; send it; propose a library change if the gap will recur.
- **Output:** an approved answer.

### T12. Resolve a conflict
- **Job profile:** Head of Clinical Governance.
- **Steps:** decide which document governs; record the ruling; ask owners to amend; issue a safety notice if staff may have acted on the wrong text.
- **Output:** a recorded ruling.

### T13. Weekly sample
- **Job profile:** Clinical Policy Reviewer.
- **Steps:** check 5% of answers and every medicines answer: screen applied, every statement cited, numbers exact, no patient-specific advice.
- **Output:** the weekly sample report.

### T14. Monthly review-date report
- **Job profile:** Clinical Governance Officer.
- **Output:** a list of Under review documents sent to their owners and the Head of Clinical Governance.

## 12. Decision rules for an answer

### R1. Who decides
The service decides nothing about a patient. The treating clinician decides care. Only the Head of Clinical Governance decides what is in the library.

### R2. Urgent first
A question that describes a possibly deteriorating patient gets the escalation route and nothing else.

### R3. What an answer may contain
Only what Published documents in force say, each statement cited, numbers quoted exactly.

### R4. No calculation, no patient advice
The service does not calculate doses or adapt a policy to a patient.

### R5. Conflicts and gaps
A newer document governs only where it says it supersedes the older one. Otherwise the point is escalated and not answered.

### R6. Language
Answers follow the staff member's language. If translations differ, the English text governs and the difference is logged.

### R7. Statuses and what they mean
As in section 6.4.

## 13. Questions staff ask, and the answer under this SOP

### Q1. What early warning score needs the rapid response team?
7 or more: call 2222 at once (CP-ESC-01 v1, section 4).

### Q2. My patient's score is 6 and they look worse. What should I do?
Follow CP-ESC-01: inform the responsible doctor now for review within 30 minutes; if the score reaches 7, call 2222.

### Q3. What dose of this antibiotic should I give a 14 kg child?
The service does not calculate doses for a patient. FORM-2026 gives the dosing entry; the prescriber and pharmacist decide.

### Q4. Which isolation precautions apply to suspected measles?
The answer quotes CP-IPC-04 with its citation.

### Q5. Is there a policy on using personal phones in clinical areas?
No Published document covers it; the question has been sent to the Clinical Governance Officer.
