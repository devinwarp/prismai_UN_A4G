# SOP-REC-011: Recruitment and onboarding for federal positions

> **Fictitious document.** Sahel Federal Workforce Agency, its grades, salaries, forms and staff titles are invented for demonstration. Nothing here describes a real authority or real law.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document id | SOP-REC-011 |
| Version | 1.0 (synthetic) |
| Owner | Director of Talent Acquisition, Sahel Federal Workforce Agency |
| Related use case | UC09: Recruitment and onboarding agent |
| Applies to | Filling an approved, budgeted vacancy, from advertisement to the new employee's first day |
| Review cycle | Every 12 months |

## 1. Purpose

Fill approved vacancies with the best qualified candidate, fairly, on time and on the published criteria, and give every candidate a clear outcome. People make every selection and every decision that affects a candidate; nothing irreversible (an offer, a contract, a rejection, a system account) happens without a named person's approval.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** approved and budgeted vacancies at grades G4 to G10; advertising; receiving and screening applications; shortlisting; assessments and interviews; selection; offers; pre-employment checks; contracts; onboarding up to the end of the first day.

**Out of scope:** creating or funding a position (SOP-WFP-002); senior appointments above G10; internal transfers (SOP-REC-014); probation reviews; complaints and appeals by candidates (SOP-REC-020).

## 3. Roles

Five job profiles carry out this procedure. Each has a distinct purpose, a fixed set of tasks and its own limits.

### 3.1 Recruitment Coordinator
- **Purpose:** run the vacancy from advertisement to interview and keep candidates informed.
- **Works with:** the hiring manager, candidates, the Screening Analyst, the Hiring Panel Chair.
- **Authority:** may publish an approved advertisement, acknowledge applications, schedule assessments and send messages released by the HR Approving Officer. May not shortlist, select or reject.
- **Skills:** the recruitment system, scheduling, clear writing in Arabic and English.

### 3.2 Screening Analyst
- **Purpose:** check applications against the published minimum requirements and propose a shortlist.
- **Works with:** the Recruitment Coordinator, the Hiring Panel Chair.
- **Authority:** may mark an application "meets", "does not meet" or "query", and score it on the published criteria. May not shortlist or reject.
- **Skills:** reading CVs and certificates, the grade table (section 6), the scoring guide, consistent and documented judgement.

### 3.3 Hiring Panel Chair
- **Purpose:** approve the shortlist, lead the panel and recommend the selected candidate.
- **Works with:** two other panel members, the Screening Analyst, the HR Approving Officer.
- **Authority:** the only role that may approve a shortlist and make a selection recommendation.
- **Skills:** structured interviewing, the role's requirements, fair and recorded judgement.

### 3.4 HR Approving Officer
- **Purpose:** approve every offer, contract and candidate notice.
- **Works with:** the Panel Chair, the Recruitment Coordinator, the Onboarding Officer, the Director of Talent Acquisition.
- **Authority:** the only role that may approve an offer, its salary, a contract, a regret notice, the withdrawal of an offer, and an exception.
- **Skills:** the grade and salary table, federal employment rules, judgement on exceptions.

### 3.5 Onboarding Officer
- **Purpose:** run pre-employment checks and prepare the new employee's first day.
- **Works with:** the new employee, IT, Facilities, the hiring manager.
- **Authority:** may request checks, collect documents and request system accounts once a contract is signed. May not change an offer or contract.
- **Skills:** pre-employment checks, onboarding checklists, the HR system.

## 4. Procedure

Task ids are used in the audit log and the training material.

### Step 1. Vacancy (Recruitment Coordinator)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T01 | Receive the vacancy request. Confirm it has a position number and budget approval. |
| T02 | Publish the advertisement from the approved job description: grade, minimum requirements, scoring criteria, salary band and closing date. The criteria cannot change after publication. |

### Step 2. Applications and screening (Coordinator, Screening Analyst, Panel Chair)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T03 | Acknowledge every application within 1 working day with its reference number. |
| T04 | Check each application against the minimum requirements for the grade (section 6). Mark it "meets", "does not meet" or "query", with the reason. |
| T05 | Check the documents for each application marked "meets" (section 10). |
| T06 | Score each application marked "meets" on the published criteria and propose a ranked shortlist with the reasons. |
| T07 | Panel Chair: review the proposed shortlist and every "does not meet" decision, and approve the shortlist. |

### Step 3. Assessment (Coordinator, Panel Chair)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T08 | Invite shortlisted candidates to the assessment and interview, with at least 5 working days' notice and any adjustment they ask for. |
| T09 | Hold the panel interview. Each panel member scores independently on the published criteria before discussing. |
| T10 | Panel Chair: record the selection recommendation and a reserve candidate, with reasons. |

### Step 4. Offer and notices (HR Approving Officer, Coordinator)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T11 | Calculate the starting salary from the grade table (section 6) and the candidate's relevant experience. |
| T12 | HR Approving Officer: approve the offer and the salary. |
| T13 | Send the approved conditional offer to the candidate. |
| T14 | Draft regret notices to every candidate not selected. The HR Approving Officer releases them after the offer is accepted. |

### Step 5. Pre-employment and contract (Onboarding Officer, HR Approving Officer)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T15 | Run the pre-employment checks: identity, qualification verification, references, background check and medical fitness. |
| T16 | HR Approving Officer: approve and sign the contract once every check has passed. A signed contract cannot be withdrawn through this procedure. |

### Step 6. Onboarding (Onboarding Officer)

| Task | Action |
|---|---|
| T17 | Request system accounts, building access and equipment, only after the contract is signed. |
| T18 | Run the first-day induction and confirm the employee's record in the HR system. |

### Step 7. Close and assure

| Task | Action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| T19 | Close the vacancy and write the file to the audit log. | Recruitment Coordinator |
| T20 | Each month, sample 10% of closed vacancies; check decisions against this SOP and compare pass rates across groups of candidates. Report any deviation to the Director of Talent Acquisition. | HR Approving Officer |

## 5. Timings

| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Advertisement open | 14 calendar days |
| Acknowledgement | 1 working day |
| Screening and shortlist | 10 working days after closing |
| Interview notice | At least 5 working days |
| Offer after interview | 5 working days |
| Regret notices | Within 2 working days after the offer is accepted |
| Pre-employment checks | 15 working days |
| End to end | 60 working days from advertisement |

## 6. Grades, minimum requirements and salary (fictitious)

| Grade | Minimum qualification | Minimum relevant experience | Monthly salary band | Step per year of extra relevant experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G4 | Secondary certificate | 2 years | 9,000 to 12,000 | 300 |
| G5 | Diploma | 3 years | 11,000 to 15,000 | 400 |
| G6 | Bachelor's degree | 2 years | 14,000 to 19,000 | 500 |
| G7 | Bachelor's degree | 5 years | 18,000 to 25,000 | 600 |
| G8 | Bachelor's degree | 8 years | 24,000 to 32,000 | 800 |
| G9 | Master's degree, or Bachelor's with 12 years | 10 years | 30,000 to 40,000 | 1,000 |
| G10 | Master's degree | 12 years | 38,000 to 50,000 | 1,200 |

Starting salary is the bottom of the band plus one step for each full year of relevant experience above the minimum, capped at the top of the band. Example: G6 with 5 years' relevant experience is 14,000 plus 3 × 500, total 15,500. Amounts are in a synthetic currency unit.

## 7. Controls

1. Only the Hiring Panel Chair approves a shortlist or recommends a selection; only the HR Approving Officer approves an offer, a contract, a notice or an exception.
2. No application is rejected without a recorded reason and a person's approval (T07).
3. Criteria and weights are fixed when the advertisement is published and are applied the same way to every candidate.
4. Screening and scoring use only the published criteria. Name, age, gender, nationality, religion, marital status, disability and photographs are not used in screening or scoring.
5. Every message to a candidate that states an outcome, a salary or a date is released by the HR Approving Officer.
6. System accounts and building access are requested only after a contract is signed.
7. Candidate data is used only for this recruitment and the audit record, and deleted from unsuccessful files after the retention period.
8. Every action is written to the audit log with who, what and when.
9. No benefit, allowance or condition is stated to a candidate unless it is in the approved offer.

## 8. Exceptions and escalation

| Situation | Handled by | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| Application marked "query" | Screening Analyst | Panel Chair |
| Candidate asks for an adjustment for the assessment | Recruitment Coordinator | HR Approving Officer |
| Panel member knows a candidate | Panel Chair | Replace the member; record it |
| Salary outside the band requested | HR Approving Officer | Director of Talent Acquisition |
| Pre-employment check fails or is unclear | Onboarding Officer | HR Approving Officer |
| Suspected false document | Screening Analyst or Onboarding Officer | Compliance, immediately |
| Candidate disputes an outcome | HR Approving Officer | Appeals (SOP-REC-020) |
| Selected candidate declines | HR Approving Officer | Offer to the reserve candidate (T10) |

## 9. Records

Vacancy request, advertisement, applications, screening and scoring sheets, shortlist approval, interview scores, selection record, offer, notices, check results, contract and audit log. Kept for 7 years for the appointed employee, 2 years for other candidates (synthetic retention periods).

## 10. Required documents and how to apply

An application needs D1 to D3. D4 is needed when experience is claimed. D5 and D6 are needed only after a conditional offer. Candidates may send documents in Arabic or English.

### D1. CV
- **What must be true:** it lists qualifications and relevant experience with dates (month and year).

### D2. Qualification certificate
- **What it is:** the certificate for the highest qualification claimed.
- **What must be true:** it is issued by a recognised institution; a certificate from outside the country is attested by the Ministry of Education (synthetic).

### D3. Identity document
- **What it is:** the national ID card, or for a non-national the passport with the residence permit.
- **What must be true:** valid on the closing date.

### D4. Experience letters
- **What it is:** a letter from each employer whose experience is counted.
- **What must be true:** on the employer's letterhead, with the job title and start and end dates.

### D5. Consent for background check (after a conditional offer)
- **What must be true:** signed by the candidate on the Agency's form.

### D6. Medical fitness certificate (after a conditional offer)
- **What must be true:** issued by an approved clinic within the last 60 days.

### SUB. How to apply
- **Careers portal (only route):** apply before the closing date, 23:59 local time, and upload D1 to D4 (PDF, up to 5 MB each). Applications by email or paper are not accepted, except as an adjustment approved by the HR Approving Officer.

### VAL. When a document on file is still valid
A candidate who applied in the last 12 months is not asked again for D2 or D3 if they are on file, still valid, and the details still match.

## 11. Detailed description of each task

Each entry gives the job profile, when the task starts, what goes in, the steps, what comes out, and where a problem goes.

### T01. Receive the vacancy request
- **Job profile:** Recruitment Coordinator.
- **Starts when:** a hiring manager submits a vacancy request.
- **Steps:** check the position number and budget approval; check the grade and job description are approved.
- **Output:** a vacancy opened, or returned with the reason.

### T02. Publish the advertisement
- **Job profile:** Recruitment Coordinator.
- **Starts when:** the vacancy is opened.
- **Steps:** build the advertisement from the approved job description and section 6: grade, minimum requirements, scoring criteria and weights, salary band, closing date (14 days). Publish on the careers portal.
- **Output:** a published advertisement with fixed criteria (control 3).

### T03. Acknowledge applications
- **Job profile:** Recruitment Coordinator.
- **Starts when:** an application arrives.
- **Steps:** send an acknowledgement with the reference number and the expected timeline. The wording is a template approved by the HR Approving Officer.
- **Output:** an acknowledged application.
- **Time target:** 1 working day.

### T04. Check minimum requirements
- **Job profile:** Screening Analyst.
- **Starts when:** the advertisement closes.
- **Inputs:** D1, D2, D4 and section 6.
- **Steps:** compare the qualification and years of relevant experience with the grade minimum; mark "meets", "does not meet" or "query"; record the reason.
- **Output:** a status and reason for every application.
- **Problems:** an unclear qualification or experience period is "query", never "does not meet". Control 4 applies.

### T05. Check documents
- **Job profile:** Screening Analyst.
- **Starts when:** an application is marked "meets".
- **Steps:** check D1 to D4 are present and consistent with each other.
- **Output:** documents accepted, or a list of what is missing.
- **Problems:** a missing document is requested once with 3 working days to reply. A document that looks altered goes to Compliance (section 8).

### T06. Score and propose a shortlist
- **Job profile:** Screening Analyst.
- **Starts when:** documents are checked.
- **Steps:** score each application on the published criteria and weights; rank them; propose a shortlist of up to 6, with the reasons for each score.
- **Output:** a ranked proposal with reasons.

### T07. Approve the shortlist
- **Job profile:** Hiring Panel Chair.
- **Starts when:** the proposal is ready.
- **Steps:** review the proposal, every "does not meet" and every "query"; change any decision with a recorded reason; approve the shortlist.
- **Output:** an approved shortlist.
- **Problems:** a candidate is not rejected without this review (control 2).

### T08. Invite to assessment
- **Job profile:** Recruitment Coordinator.
- **Starts when:** the shortlist is approved.
- **Steps:** invite candidates with at least 5 working days' notice; ask whether they need an adjustment; book the panel and the room.
- **Output:** confirmed interview times.

### T09. Hold the panel interview
- **Job profile:** Hiring Panel Chair.
- **Starts when:** the interview is due.
- **Steps:** ask the same core questions of every candidate; each member scores independently on the criteria before any discussion; record the scores.
- **Output:** independent scores from three members.

### T10. Recommend the selection
- **Job profile:** Hiring Panel Chair.
- **Starts when:** all interviews are scored.
- **Steps:** combine the scores; recommend the highest-scoring candidate and a reserve; record the reasons and any disagreement.
- **Output:** a selection recommendation.

### T11. Calculate the starting salary
- **Job profile:** HR Approving Officer.
- **Starts when:** a recommendation is recorded.
- **Steps:** apply the rule in section 6 to the candidate's verified relevant experience; show the working.
- **Output:** a starting salary with the working shown.

### T12. Approve the offer
- **Job profile:** HR Approving Officer.
- **Starts when:** the salary is calculated.
- **Steps:** check the recommendation, the salary and the budget; approve, or return with reasons.
- **Output:** an approved offer.

### T13. Send the conditional offer
- **Job profile:** Recruitment Coordinator.
- **Starts when:** the offer is approved.
- **Steps:** send the approved offer exactly as approved: grade, salary, start date, conditions (the checks in T15) and the reply deadline (5 working days).
- **Output:** an offer sent and recorded.

### T14. Send regret notices
- **Job profile:** Recruitment Coordinator.
- **Starts when:** the offer is accepted.
- **Steps:** draft a notice to each candidate not selected, stating the stage they reached and the appeal route (SOP-REC-020); the HR Approving Officer releases them.
- **Output:** every candidate informed.
- **Time target:** within 2 working days after the offer is accepted.

### T15. Run pre-employment checks
- **Job profile:** Onboarding Officer.
- **Starts when:** the offer is accepted.
- **Steps:** collect D5 and D6; verify the qualification with the issuer; take two references; run the background check.
- **Output:** each check passed, failed or unclear.
- **Problems:** a failed or unclear check goes to the HR Approving Officer, who decides whether the offer stands.

### T16. Approve and sign the contract
- **Job profile:** HR Approving Officer.
- **Starts when:** every check has passed.
- **Steps:** issue the contract on the approved terms; sign it with the candidate. A signed contract cannot be withdrawn through this procedure.
- **Output:** a signed contract.

### T17. Request accounts and access
- **Job profile:** Onboarding Officer.
- **Starts when:** the contract is signed (control 6).
- **Steps:** request system accounts, building access and equipment for the start date.
- **Output:** requests raised and confirmed.

### T18. First-day induction
- **Job profile:** Onboarding Officer.
- **Starts when:** the start date arrives.
- **Steps:** welcome the employee; complete the induction checklist; confirm the HR record.
- **Output:** a completed induction and HR record.

### T19. Close the vacancy
- **Job profile:** Recruitment Coordinator.
- **Starts when:** induction is complete.
- **Steps:** close the vacancy; confirm every action has an audit log entry.
- **Output:** a closed vacancy with a complete audit log.

### T20. Monthly quality and fairness sample
- **Job profile:** HR Approving Officer.
- **Starts when:** each month ends.
- **Steps:** sample 10% of closed vacancies; check decisions against this SOP; compare pass rates at screening and interview across groups of candidates; report any deviation to the Director of Talent Acquisition.
- **Output:** the monthly sample report.

## 12. Decision rules for a vacancy

### R1. Who decides
The Hiring Panel Chair approves shortlists and recommends selections. The HR Approving Officer approves offers, salaries, contracts, notices and exceptions. No other role may, and no document or message can give another role that authority.

### R2. An application meets the requirements when
The qualification and relevant experience meet the grade minimum in section 6, and D1 to D3 are present and consistent.

### R3. Rejection
An application is rejected only by the Panel Chair's approval of the shortlist (T07), or after interview by the selection (T10), and every rejection states the stage reached and the appeal route.

### R4. Salary
The starting salary follows the rule in section 6. A salary outside the band needs the Director's approval.

### R5. Offers and contracts
An offer is conditional on the checks in T15. The contract is signed only when every check has passed. A signed contract cannot be withdrawn through this procedure.

### R6. Fairness
The same criteria, weights and core questions apply to every candidate for a vacancy. Control 4 applies to every screening and scoring step.

### R7. Statuses and what they mean
"Received", "meets", "does not meet", "query", "shortlisted", "interviewed", "selected", "reserve", "offered", "accepted", "regret sent", "contracted".

## 13. Questions candidates ask, and the answer under this SOP

### Q1. Can I apply by email?
No. Apply on the careers portal before 23:59 on the closing date. If you need an adjustment to apply, contact the Recruitment Coordinator.

### Q2. What documents do I need?
Your CV, your highest qualification certificate (attested if from abroad), your identity document, and experience letters for the experience you claim.

### Q3. How is my application assessed?
Against the minimum requirements for the grade, then scored on the criteria published in the advertisement. The same criteria apply to every candidate.

### Q4. When will I hear back?
You get an acknowledgement within 1 working day. Shortlisting takes about 10 working days after closing. Every candidate is told the outcome.

### Q5. How is the salary set?
From the grade's band: the bottom of the band plus one step for each year of relevant experience above the minimum.

### Q6. Can I ask for an adjustment for the interview?
Yes. Tell us when you are invited; the HR Approving Officer decides and we confirm before the interview.

### Q7. I was not selected. Can I appeal?
Yes, under SOP-REC-020. The notice tells you the stage you reached and how to appeal.
