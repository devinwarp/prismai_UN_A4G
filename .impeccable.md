## Design Context

### Users
Federal AI-governance officers and entity AI leads who review portfolio
compliance against the UAE federal 50%-agentic target (by 2028-04-30), plus
hackathon judges watching a 7-minute demo. They both read and configure: set
the policy, import use cases, tune per-entity human-in-the-loop rules, release
scenario steps, run pilots. They must trust every verdict and see why: which
gate, which evidence, which record in the hash chain. Judges need the headline
(progress, gap, verdict mix) in seconds; officers drill into the cause.

### Brand Personality
Audit file. Calm, credible, quietly precise. Prism here is the governance
ledger for agentic AI: verdicts are never colour alone, every claim shows its
evidence, and "simulated" is always visibly labelled.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial on warm paper: paper/ink neutrals, pine-green "lit" accent, ember
for problems. Spectral (display), Geist (body), JetBrains Mono for small
uppercase labels. Light and dark themes both exist — every colour must come
from the `--color-*` tokens in `globals.css`, never a hard-coded hex.
Anti-references: SaaS KPI dashboards, neon-on-dark, gradient accents.

### Design Principles
1. Summary first, detail on demand — show the top of a distribution, let the
   reader expand the long tail (top-N + "show all" + filter).
2. Every chart answers a question; no decorative sparklines.
3. Charts are entry points — clicking a segment or legend item filters the detail table.
4. Accent colour is rare: lit marks selection/positive, ember marks pain.
5. Keyboard and reduced-motion parity with mouse.
